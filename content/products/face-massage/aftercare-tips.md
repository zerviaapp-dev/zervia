# Aftercare Tips — After Your Session & Daily Skin Habits

*Keep the glow going long after you leave your appointment.*

---

## Right After Your Session

**Don't wash your face immediately.** The oils and serums applied during your session are doing good work — let them absorb for at least 2–4 hours, or leave them on until your evening cleanse.

**Skip heavy makeup for the rest of the day.** Your skin is calm and open; give it a breather. If you must wear makeup, keep it light.

**Drink water.** Facial massage encourages fluid movement — staying hydrated supports your body's natural processes afterward.

**Avoid intense heat.** Skip the sauna, steam room, or very hot shower for a few hours. Lukewarm is your friend.

**Expect mild redness.** A slight flush after gua sha or massage is normal and usually fades within an hour. If redness persists beyond a few hours or you feel irritation, apply a cool compress and let us know at your next visit.

---

## The First 24 Hours

- **Hands off your face.** Try not to touch or pick at your skin — your hands carry bacteria.
- **Sleep on a clean pillowcase.** Fresh pillowcases 2–3 times a week make a real difference for skin clarity.
- **Hold off on harsh actives.** Skip strong exfoliants, retinoids, or high-concentration acids the evening after your session; resume your normal routine the next day unless your skin feels sensitive.

---

## Daily Habits for Healthy, Glowing Skin

These small habits do more for your skin over time than any single treatment:

**1. Practice your routine.** Five minutes of lymphatic drainage or jade rolling each morning — the routine from your personalized plan — keeps puffiness down and circulation up.

**2. Hydrate inside and out.** Drink water through the day and moisturize while your skin is still slightly damp to lock in hydration.

**3. Sunscreen every day.** UV damage is the #1 driver of premature aging and dullness — more than any other factor. A broad-spectrum SPF 30+ every morning, even on cloudy Calgary winter days, is the single best thing you can do for your skin.

**4. Sleep on your back when you can.** Sleeping face-down pushes fluid into the face overnight and causes morning puffiness. A silk or satin pillowcase reduces friction and is gentler on skin.

**5. Manage salt and alcohol.** Both cause fluid retention. If you wake up puffy, think about last night's dinner before blaming your skincare.

**6. Cold therapy for puffy mornings.** A chilled jade roller, cold spoons, or a cool damp cloth for a few minutes works wonders on morning swelling.

**7. Keep your tools clean.** Wash gua sha stones and rollers with mild soap after every use. Dirty tools are a fast track to breakouts.

**8. Be consistent with products.** Switching products every week confuses your skin. Give a new product 4–6 weeks before judging it, and introduce only one new product at a time.

---

## Between Sessions

- **Track what you notice.** Jot down how your skin looks and feels in the week after each session — puffiness levels, texture, how your makeup sits. This helps us fine-tune your plan.
- **Book maintenance every 2–4 weeks.** Regular sessions build on each other; sporadic ones start from scratch each time.
- **Tell us about changes.** New medications, new skincare products, seasonal allergies, extra stress — all of it affects your skin. Mention it at your next consultation so we can adapt.

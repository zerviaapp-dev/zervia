## FAQ

**Does facial massage actually help with puffiness?**
Many clients notice a visible difference after a single session — a lighter, more sculpted look, especially around the eyes and jawline. Lymphatic drainage techniques are designed to encourage fluid movement away from areas where it collects, which is what causes that morning puffiness. Results are typically most noticeable right after the session and build with regular practice. We make no medical claims — this is a cosmetic wellness service, and individual results vary.

**How often should I book sessions?**
For most people, once every 2–4 weeks works well as maintenance, with weekly sessions for the first month if you're targeting noticeable puffiness or tension. Between sessions, your personalized home routine (5–10 minutes a day) keeps the benefits going. Your consultation includes an honest recommendation based on your goals — never pressure to over-book.

**I have sensitive skin. Is this safe for me?**
Usually, yes. All techniques are gentle, and we use fragrance-free, skin-friendly oils chosen for sensitivity. Please tell us about any allergies, eczema, rosacea, or skin conditions in your consultation so we can adapt the treatment. If your skin is currently irritated, broken out, or infected, we'll recommend waiting until it calms down.

**What should I bring to my session?**
Just yourself! We provide everything — oils, tools, towels, and a comfortable space. Come with a clean, makeup-free face if you can (we'll cleanse you if not). For virtual sessions, have a mirror, a facial oil or serum, and — if you have them — a gua sha stone or jade roller handy; we'll work with whatever you've got.

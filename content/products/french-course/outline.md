# Learn French — Full Curriculum Outline

**Course:** Learn French (taught in English, for English speakers)
**Level:** Absolute beginner → conversational
**Approach:** Practical, conversation-first. Grammar is introduced only where it helps you speak. Every lesson ends with something you can say out loud in a real situation.

---

## Module 1 — Sound It Right: Pronunciation & the Alphabet
- **1.1** The French alphabet and how letters actually sound — the ones that will trick you (r, u, gn, ch)
- **1.2** The famous French R — a step-by-step way to make it without spraining your throat
- **1.3** Nasal vowels (an, en, on, in, un) — the sounds English doesn't have, simplified
- **1.4** Silent letters and liaisons — why "ils parlent" doesn't sound like it's spelled, and when to link words
- **1.5** Accents that change meaning — é, è, ê, à vs a, ç — small marks, big difference
- **1.6** Sounding natural — rhythm, stress, and the French habit of gliding through sentences

## Module 2 — Greetings & Introductions
- **2.1** Essential greetings — Bonjour, Bonsoir, Salut, Bonne journée and when to use each
- **2.2** Introducing yourself — Je m'appelle…, Je suis…, Moi c'est…
- **2.3** Politeness basics — Merci, S'il vous plaît, De rien, Excusez-moi
- **2.4** Tu vs vous — the two "you"s and how not to accidentally offend someone
- **2.5** Small talk openers — Comment ça va ? Ça va bien ? and how to answer honestly
- **2.6** Saying goodbye — Au revoir, À bientôt, À demain, Bonne soirée

## Module 3 — Numbers, Time & Dates
- **3.1** Numbers 0–20 and 21–100 — including the fun logic of "four-twenties" (quatre-vingts)
- **3.2** Telling the time — Quelle heure est-il ? plus "half past" and "quarter to" the French way
- **3.3** Days, months, and dates — C'est quand ton anniversaire ?
- **3.4** Big numbers, phone numbers, and prices — reading prices aloud without panic
- **3.5** Making appointments — Demain à dix heures, la semaine prochaine

## Module 4 — Food & Dining
- **4.1** Ordering at a café — Un café, s'il vous plaît, and the coffee vocabulary that matters
- **4.2** Reading a French menu — les entrées, les plats, les desserts, and common dishes decoded
- **4.3** At a restaurant — asking for a table, ordering, asking for the bill
- **4.4** Dietary needs — Je suis végétarien(ne), Je suis allergique à…
- **4.5** Grocery shopping and markets — quantities, weights, and market-day phrases
- **4.6** Compliments and complaints — C'est délicieux, and how to politely flag a problem

## Module 5 — Shopping & Money
- **5.1** Browsing a shop — Je regarde seulement (just looking), Est-ce que je peux essayer ?
- **5.2** Sizes, colors, and prices — Ça coûte combien ? C'est trop cher ?
- **5.3** Paying — cash, card, and understanding the receipt
- **5.4** Returns and exchanges — Je voudrais échanger ceci
- **5.5** Bargaining politely — where it's okay (markets) and where it's not

## Module 6 — Travel & Directions
- **6.1** At the airport and station — arrivals, departures, delays, and platform announcements
- **6.2** Taxis and rides — to the address, how much, and confirming the route
- **6.3** Asking for directions — Où est… ? plus understanding the answers (à gauche, tout droit)
- **6.4** Hotels — checking in, room problems, checking out
- **6.5** Emergencies on the road — lost passports, missed trains, and who to ask

## Module 7 — Family & Social Life
- **7.1** Talking about family — mon père, ma sœur, and describing relationships
- **7.2** Making friends — invitations, suggesting plans, accepting or declining gracefully
- **7.3** Talking about hobbies — J'aime…, Je fais du sport, and weekend chat
- **7.4** Compliments and celebrations — Joyeux anniversaire, Félicitations, wedding and holiday wishes
- **7.5** Texting and messaging like a local — common abbreviations and casual tone

## Module 8 — Work & Business Phrases
- **8.1** Professional introductions — Enchanté, your title, and your company
- **8.2** Meetings — asking questions, understanding agendas, and polite interruptions
- **8.3** Phone calls and voicemails — Allô, leaving a message, spelling your name aloud
- **8.4** Emails and formal closings — Cordialement, Bien à vous, and when formality matters
- **8.5** Networking small talk — Quel est votre métier ? and keeping the conversation alive

## Module 9 — Emergencies & Health
- **9.1** Getting help — Au secours ! J'ai besoin d'aide, calling emergency numbers (15, 17, 18, 112)
- **9.2** At the doctor — describing symptoms, J'ai mal à…, Je suis malade
- **9.3** At the pharmacy — asking for medicine, understanding dosages
- **9.4** Describing accidents — what happened, where it hurts, insurance phrases
- **9.5** Important documents — passport, insurance, and explaining your situation simply

## Module 10 — Culture, Etiquette & Real Conversation
- **10.1** Tu vs vous, deep dive — when to switch, and how the French navigate it themselves
- **10.2** La bise — the cheek-kiss greeting: when, how many, and how not to awkwardly headbutt someone
- **10.3** Table manners and social rules — hands on the table, bread etiquette, and the art of the apéro
- **10.4** Conversation fillers natives use — alors, bon, en fait, du coup — sound natural instantly
- **10.5** Handling misunderstandings — Pouvez-vous répéter ? Plus lentement, s'il vous plaît
- **10.6** Your first real conversation — a guided practice pulling everything together

# Learn French — Sales Copy

**Subtitle:** A beginner-to-conversational French course for English speakers — practical, warm, and built for real life.

---

## Headline
Speak French with confidence — starting with your very first lesson.

## The promise (honest, no hype)
This course won't make you fluent in 30 days — no honest course can promise that. What it *will* do is get you speaking real French from day one: greeting people correctly, ordering food without pointing at the menu, asking for directions, and holding simple conversations. Conversation by conversation, you'll build the kind of French that works in a café in Paris, at a market in Montréal, or on a video call with a French-speaking colleague.

## Who this is for
- Total beginners who've never spoken a word of French
- Travelers who want more than a phrasebook — they want to actually talk
- Professionals who work with French-speaking colleagues or clients
- Anyone who's tried apps and flashcards but still freezes when a real person speaks to them

## What makes it different

**Conversation first, grammar second.** You'll learn French the way you use it: in greetings, at restaurants, in shops, on the road. Grammar shows up only when it helps you say something — never as a dry drill.

**Pronunciation you can actually do.** French sounds — the nasal vowels, the silent letters, the famous "r" — broken down step by step with simple phonetics you can practice at your kitchen table. No phonetic-alphabet degree required.

**Cultural notes that save you embarrassment.** When do you use *tu* and when is it *vous*? How many cheek kisses is "la bise," and when do you skip it entirely? These are the things phrasebooks never teach — and the things that make French speakers warm up to you.

**Real-life scenarios, not textbook dialogues.** Ordering coffee. Checking into a hotel. Small talk at a work event. A trip to the pharmacy. Every lesson is built around a situation you'll actually be in.

## What's inside
10 modules, 50+ lessons, taking you from the alphabet and your first *Bonjour* to handling travel, dining, shopping, social life, work situations, and emergencies — plus the cultural fluency to do it all with confidence.

## What you'll be able to do
- Greet and introduce yourself properly (and know when to use *tu* vs *vous*)
- Order food and drinks, read a menu, and handle the bill
- Ask for and understand directions
- Shop, ask prices, and pay without stress
- Make small talk — about family, work, hobbies, weekends
- Handle travel basics: airports, hotels, taxis
- Get help in an emergency and see a doctor or pharmacist
- Understand French social etiquette — from la bise to table manners

## The honest part
Learning a language takes time and practice. This course gives you the words, the sounds, the cultural know-how, and the confidence to start — and a clear path to keep going. Show up, practice out loud, and you'll be amazed how far "just" greetings and café orders take you.

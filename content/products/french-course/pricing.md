# Learn French — Pricing

## Suggested tiers

| Tier | Price | What they get |
|---|---|---|
| **Single course** | **$39** | The full Learn French course (all 10 modules, 50+ lessons) |
| **3-course bundle** | **$89** | Learn French + any 2 other language courses |
| **All 9 languages** | **$199** | Every language course in the Zervia collection |

## Rationale

- **$39 single-course price** sits comfortably inside the standard $29–$49 band for a self-paced beginner ecourse. It's low enough to be an impulse-friendly decision, and high enough to signal real substance (not a free pamphlet).
- **$89 for 3 courses** creates a clear step-up: at ~$30 per course it's a genuine discount versus buying three singles ($117), which nudges buyers toward the bundle while protecting margin.
- **$199 for all 9 languages** is the anchor offer — under $23 per language, it makes the full collection feel like the obvious best value for serious learners and polyglot-curious buyers, and it raises average order value.
- The ladder ($39 → $89 → $199) follows a classic good-better-best structure: each tier is meaningfully cheaper per course, so upgrading always feels like the smart move.

# Speak Mandarin — Pricing

## Recommended price tiers

| Tier | Price | Contents |
|------|-------|----------|
| **Single course** | **$49** | Speak Mandarin — full 10-module course, lifetime access |
| **3-course bundle** | **$109** | Speak Mandarin + any 2 other Zervia language courses |
| **All 9 languages** | **$249** | Complete Zervia language library — every current and future language course |

## Rationale

- **$49 single-course price (top of the $29–49 range):** Mandarin commands the highest perceived value of any language course. Learners already expect it to be the "hard, serious" language — pricing it at the premium end matches that expectation and signals quality. Pricing it *below* the range would feel suspicious for a language with this reputation; pricing above $49 would push it out of impulse-buy territory.
- **$109 three-course bundle:** Positions Mandarin as the anchor of a bundle (most buyers will pair it with a "fun" language like French or Spanish). At ~$36/course it feels like a genuine deal while keeping per-course revenue healthy. Classic anchor: the $49 single makes $109 for three feel like the smart choice.
- **$249 all-9-languages:** The "polyglot dream" tier. At under $28/language it's the obvious value play for serious learners, and it maximizes customer lifetime value in a single transaction. Also future-proofs the library — buyers get new language courses as they're released.
- **No subscription:** One-time pricing keeps it simple and matches buyer expectations for ecourses. Lifetime access is the selling point — say it everywhere.

## FAQ

**Do I need a degree to take this course?**
No. This course assumes zero engineering background. Everything is explained in plain language, step by step. It's designed for curious beginners, not engineering students.

**Will this make me an engineer?**
No — and we'd be doing you a disservice to suggest otherwise. Becoming a licensed engineer requires an accredited degree, professional experience, and licensing exams. What this course gives you is genuine foundational knowledge: you'll understand drawings, concrete, measurement, and inspection the way working professionals think about them. That's valuable whether you're managing a home build, starting a construction career, or just want to understand the built world.

**Is this useful if I'm managing my own home build or renovation?**
Yes — this is one of the best reasons to take it. Homeowners who can read their own drawings, understand what concrete work should look like, and know the basic quality checkpoints are dramatically harder to overcharge or mislead. You'll communicate with your contractor in their language and catch issues while they're cheap to fix.

**Can I ask questions as I go?**
Yes. The course includes a community Q&A format — post your questions and get answers from the instructor team, and learn from other students' questions too. Drawing-reading questions are especially welcome; snap a photo of a detail you're stuck on and we'll walk through it.

## FAQ

**Does it hurt?**
Not at all. Every technique we use is intentionally gentle — the under-eye area is delicate, and our approach respects that. Most clients describe it as deeply relaxing; many nearly fall asleep during the lymphatic massage.

**How many sessions will I need?**
It depends on your goals. Many clients notice they look more refreshed after a single session. For puffiness and dullness that build up over time, we typically recommend 3 sessions spaced 1–2 weeks apart, then occasional maintenance. Your consultation will include an honest recommendation — we'll never upsell you sessions you don't need.

**How long do the results last?**
You'll leave each session looking brighter and less puffy — that refreshed look typically lasts several days. With repeated sessions and good daily habits (sleep, hydration, cold compresses — see our care guide), results build and last longer. This is a beauty treatment, not a permanent fix; maintenance is part of keeping the glow.

**Is it safe for sensitive skin?**
Yes — this service was designed with sensitive skin in mind. We use fragrance-free, hypoallergenic products and gentle techniques. That said, please tell us about any allergies, eczema, rosacea, or skin conditions during your consultation so we can adapt. If you've had a recent eye procedure or infection, wait until you're fully healed.

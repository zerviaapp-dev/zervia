# Bright Eyes Refresh — Before & After Care Guide

*Part of your Zervia Bright Eyes Refresh service. Following this guide helps you get the most from each session.*

---

## Before Your Session — How to Prepare

- **Arrive makeup-free.** Come with a clean face — no foundation, concealer, or eye makeup. This lets us assess your skin accurately and avoids irritation during treatment.
- **Get decent sleep the night before.** You don't need a perfect 8 hours, but avoid arriving on zero sleep if you can — well-rested skin responds better.
- **Stay hydrated.** Drink water normally through the day; avoid showing up dehydrated.
- **Skip heavy eye creams that morning.** A light moisturizer is fine; heavy products can interfere with the treatment.
- **Let us know** about any new allergies, skin flare-ups, eye infections, or recent procedures before we begin.

---

## Aftercare — The First 48 Hours

### Do:
- Keep the area clean and moisturized with a gentle, fragrance-free eye cream
- Drink plenty of water — hydration supports the lymphatic drainage effect
- Sleep on your back with your head slightly elevated (an extra pillow) to discourage fluid pooling overnight
- Apply a cool compress for 5–10 minutes if any puffiness returns

### Avoid:
- Rubbing or tugging at your eyes (this stretches delicate skin and worsens puffiness over time)
- Heavy makeup on the under-eye area for 24 hours
- Salty foods and alcohol in excess — both encourage fluid retention
- Hot showers directed at your face, saunas, or intense heat for 24 hours

## Daily Habits That Help Reduce Puffiness

These small habits make a real difference between sessions:

1. **Prioritize sleep.** 7–8 hours is the single biggest factor. Consistent bedtimes beat catch-up sleep.
2. **Watch the salt.** High-sodium dinners (instant noodles, fast food, processed snacks) are a top cause of morning puffiness.
3. **Manage allergies.** If your eyes are often itchy or watery, seasonal allergies or dust mites may be the culprit — treat the allergy, not just the symptom.
4. **Cold compress how-to:** Wrap a few ice cubes in a clean soft cloth (or chill two spoons in the fridge for 10 minutes). Rest gently on closed eyes for 5–10 minutes in the morning. Never apply ice directly to skin.
5. **Stay hydrated through the day.** Paradoxically, dehydration makes your body hold onto water — including around the eyes.
6. **Limit screen strain.** Follow the 20-20-20 rule: every 20 minutes, look at something 20 feet away for 20 seconds. Less squinting = less strain lines.
7. **Remove makeup gently every night.** Use a mild remover; never scrub. Sleeping in makeup irritates the eye area.
8. **Sunscreen daily.** UV breaks down collagen in thin under-eye skin. A light SPF around the eyes (or sunglasses) protects long-term.

## When to See a Dermatologist Instead

Our service is cosmetic and relaxing — but some under-eye concerns are medical. **Please see a doctor or dermatologist if you notice:**

- Sudden or severe swelling of one or both eyes
- Pain, tenderness, or warmth around the eyes
- Redness, discharge, or signs of infection
- Vision changes or double vision
- Dark circles that appeared suddenly or are worsening rapidly
- A lump, bump, or growth near the eye
- Puffiness that doesn't improve at all with sleep, hydration, and time

These can signal allergies, thyroid issues, infections, or other conditions that need medical care — no beauty treatment can or should address them.

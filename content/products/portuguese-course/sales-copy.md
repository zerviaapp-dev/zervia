### Why this course is different

**🇧🇷 Brazilian Portuguese, front and center.** Most courses teach a vague mix or lean toward European Portuguese. This one is unapologetically Brazilian — the accent, the vocabulary, the expressions you'll actually hear in São Paulo, Rio, Salvador, or Fortaleza.

**🌍 With Portugal covered too.** Traveling to Lisbon or Porto? You'll find clear side notes throughout explaining how European Portuguese differs — pronunciation, vocabulary (trem vs. comboio), and grammar (estou falando vs. estou a falar). You won't be caught off guard.

**👃 Nasal sounds, made easy.** The ão in *não*, the ã in *mão* — the sounds that trip up every English speaker — are broken down into simple, physical techniques you can practice in minutes. Students consistently say this alone was worth it.

**🗣️ Real-life scenarios, not classroom sentences.** Order food, haggle at a market, check into a hotel, describe symptoms at a pharmacy, small-talk at a party. Each module ends with mini-dialogues you can role-play today.

**🤝 Culture built in.** Why Brazilians greet with a kiss on the cheek (beijinho), when to use *você* vs. *tu* vs. *o senhor*, why lunch is sacred — so you don't just speak the language, you respect the culture.

# Learn Portuguese: From Zero to Conversational
## Full Course Outline (Brazilian Portuguese focus)

**Target learner:** English speaker starting from zero.
**Approach:** Practical and conversational — short lessons you can use the same day. Grammar appears only where it makes speaking easier, never as a drill.

---

### Module 1 — Sounds of Portuguese: Pronunciation & Alphabet
- **1.1** The Portuguese alphabet & the letters English doesn't have (ç, ã, õ, é/ê, ó/ô)
- **1.2** Vowels: the open/closed trick (fala vs. fala differences that matter)
- **1.3** Nasal vowels made simple: ã, õ, an, em, in, on, um — how to hum through the sound
- **1.4** The famous -ão ending: mão, coração, não — getting it right the first time
- **1.5** The sneaky ones: lh (like million), nh (like canyon), r at word start (a breathy h), r in the middle (a soft tap)
- **1.6** Stress & rhythm: where the accent naturally falls, and why it changes meaning (papa vs. papá)

### Module 2 — Greetings & Introductions
- **2.1** The essentials: Olá, Oi, Bom dia, Boa tarde, Boa noite
- **2.2** How are you? Tudo bem? Como vai? — and honest answers beyond "fine"
- **2.3** Introductions: Meu nome é..., Prazer em conhecê-lo/a, Sou do Canadá / Sou dos Estados Unidos
- **2.4** você vs. tu vs. o senhor/a senhora: who to use where (and why Brazilian tu is mostly a nickname for você)
- **2.5** Goodbyes that sound natural: Tchau, Até logo, Até mais, Foi um prazer
- **2.6** Mini-dialogue lab: meeting someone at a party, at the office, at a family gathering

### Module 3 — Numbers, Time & Dates
- **3.1** Numbers 1–20 (with pronunciation drills)
- **3.2** Tens, hundreds, thousands: 21, 100, 1.000 — and the comma-vs-period surprise (R$ 1.000,50)
- **3.3** Telling the time: São duas horas vs. É uma hora
- **3.4** Days of the week (the numbered ones — segunda-feira, terça-feira — made easy), months, seasons
- **3.5** Dates & scheduling: Hoje, amanhã, semana que vem — making plans in Portuguese
- **3.6** Money basics: Quanto custa?, reais and centavos, reading prices aloud

### Module 4 — Food & Dining
- **4.1** Restaurant survival: Uma mesa para dois, O cardápio, por favor
- **4.2** Ordering: Quero / Gostaria de — and how to customize (sem açúcar, sem gelo)
- **4.3** Brazilian food vocabulary: feijoada, açaí, pão de queijo, brigadeiro, caipirinha, suco de maracujá
- **4.4** Dietary needs: Sou vegetariano/a, Tenho alergia a... — staying safe and polite
- **4.5** The bill & tipping: A conta, por favor — Brazil's 10% serviço explained
- **4.6** Culture note: meals as social events, the boteco, and why lunch is the big meal

### Module 5 — Shopping & Money
- **5.1** Store phrases: Quanto custa? Tem em outro tamanho/cor? Posso provar?
- **5.2** Sizes, colors, quantities: Grande, médio, pequeno, número (shoe size)
- **5.3** Negotiating politely at markets: Tem desconto? — where haggling is and isn't OK in Brazil
- **5.4** Paying: cartão, dinheiro, Pix — Brazil's payment culture
- **5.5** Helpful grammar nugget: demonstratives (este, esse, aquele) — pointing at things like a local
- **5.6** Dialogue lab: buying clothes, buying souvenirs at a feira

### Module 6 — Travel & Directions
- **6.1** Airport & transport: Onde fica...? A que horas sai? — taxi, metro, Uber in Brazil
- **6.2** Asking for directions: Como chego em...? Pode me ajudar? — understanding answers (siga reto, vire à direita)
- **6.3** Hotels: Tenho uma reserva, Que horas é o check-out? — checking in with confidence
- **6.4** Emergencies lite: Perdi meu passaporte, Preciso de um médico — calm phrases for rough moments
- **6.5** Useful grammar nugget: prepositions de/em/para in real contexts (sou de, moro em, vou para)
- **6.6** Culture note: Brazilian addresses, neighborhoods (bairros), and why asking locals beats GPS

### Module 7 — Family & Social Life
- **7.1** Family vocabulary: mãe, pai, avó, neto, sogra — describing yours: Tenho dois filhos
- **7.2** Describing people: personality & appearance adjectives (with gender agreement made simple)
- **7.3** Hobbies & plans: Gosto de... — talking about what you enjoy
- **7.4** Invitations & plans: Quer ir...? Vamos...! — making and accepting plans
- **7.5** Small talk like a Brazilian: football, weather, weekend plans
- **7.6** Culture note: Brazilian warmth — hugs, cheek kisses (beijinho), and the long goodbye
- **7.7** Dialogue lab: dinner at a Brazilian friend's house

### Module 8 — Work & Business Phrases
- **8.1** Professional introductions: Sou engenheiro/a, Trabalho com... — your role in Portuguese
- **8.2** Meetings & emails: phrases that keep you polite and clear (Com licença, Desculpe, Obrigado pela reunião)
- **8.3** Phone & video calls: Alô? Pode repetir, por favor? — surviving bad audio
- **8.4** Numbers at work: prices, deadlines, quantities (building on Module 3)
- **8.5** Culture note: Brazilian business warmth — formality, titles (Doutor/a), and building relationships before business

### Module 9 — Emergencies & Health
- **9.1** Health basics: Estou doente, Dói aqui — describing symptoms
- **9.2** At the pharmacy: Preciso de... — common needs (dor de cabeça, febre, alergia)
- **9.3** Calling for help: Chame uma ambulância, Preciso de ajuda
- **9.4** Documents: Onde fica a delegacia? Perdi minha carteira — police & lost items
- **9.5** Travel-insurance phrases & your blood type, allergies, medications — preparedness checklist

### Module 10 — Culture & Etiquette + Conversation Practice
- **10.1** você vs. tu vs. formal address: the complete map (Brazil regions + Portugal differences)
- **10.2** Brazilian Portuguese vs. European Portuguese: key pronunciation, vocabulary & grammar differences (você vs. tu, gerúndio: estou falando vs. estou a falar, comboio vs. trem)
- **10.3** Everyday etiquette: beijinho (one kiss? two? depends on the state), punctuality is flexible, never "OK" with a hand gesture
- **10.4** Conversation practice: 10 real-life scenario role-plays (café, market, hotel, office, party)
- **10.5** Your next steps: how to keep improving — apps, conversation partners, Brazilian media recommendations
- **10.6** Final quiz & celebration: certificate of completion

**Course totals:** 10 modules · 59 lessons · ~70 essential phrases · 20+ dialogues · lifetime updates.

### 1. Single course — **$39**
The full "Learn Portuguese: From Zero to Conversational" ecourse. All 10 modules, 59 lessons, dialogues, pronunciation system, and practice exercises, with lifetime access and updates.

### 2. 3-course bundle — **$89**
Any three language courses (e.g., Portuguese + Spanish + French). A natural fit for travelers, polyglot dabblers, and anyone living in a multilingual household.

### 3. All 9 languages — **$199**
The complete Zervia language library: every language ecourse we publish, current and future releases within the library. The best value for serious learners and families learning together.

## Rationale

- **$39 base** sits right in the $29–49 sweet spot for a self-paced digital ecourse. It's low enough to be an impulse purchase for a trip or a new hobby, yet high enough to signal real quality — above the "$9.99 junk course" bracket that teaches buyers to expect nothing.
- **$89 for 3** works out to ~$30/course — a genuine 25% saving that nudges single-course buyers upward without feeling like a stretch. Three is the natural "I want Portuguese plus one or two more" quantity.
- **$199 for 9** works out to ~$22/course — a 43% discount per course that makes the bundle feel like a steal, while still yielding ~$199 average revenue per committed learner. It also future-proofs the purchase ("all 9 languages"), which is a strong selling point for families and lifelong learners.
- **No subscription.** One-time payments fit this product: learners buy once, learn at their own pace, and come back for future courses instead of churning on a monthly fee.
- **No exaggerated discount anchoring** (no fake "$499 → $39" strikethroughs). The prices are honest, the bundles are genuinely cheaper per course, and that keeps trust — which is the whole brand.

# WEEK 1 — Foundations: Build the Base 🧱

**Rule for week 1:** Change as little as possible. We observe, we set up, we win small.

## Daily Targets (every day, week 1)

| Target | Goal |
|---|---|
| 💧 Water | 2–2.5 litres (about 8–10 glasses) |
| 🥗 Vegetables | At least 1 serving with lunch AND dinner |
| 😴 Sleep | 7–8 hours; consistent bedtime |
| 🚶 Steps | Move daily — no strict target yet, just notice your baseline |

## Nutrition Framework — Week 1

No dieting. No calorie counting. Just three jobs:

1. **Write down what you eat** (phone notes app is fine). No judgment, no editing — honesty is the whole point. This becomes your "before" picture.
2. **Add one vegetable** to lunch and dinner. Not instead of anything — *in addition*. Frozen mixed veg, a side salad, pinakbet, ginisang ampalaya, steamed broccoli — whatever you'll actually eat.
3. **Drink water before each meal.** One full glass, 15 minutes before eating. It aids digestion and gently curbs overeating.

### Sample Day of Eating (Filipino-friendly, week 1 style)

> *This is an example of normal eating + the week-1 additions — not a prescription.*

- **Breakfast:** Tapsilog (tapa + sinangag + itlog) + 1 glass of water before eating
- **Mid-morning:** Water; banana if hungry
- **Lunch:** Your usual ulam + rice, **plus** a side of ginisang sayote or steamed okra; water before the meal
- **Afternoon:** Water; unsweetened tea or black coffee if you like
- **Dinner:** Sinigang na isda or tinolang manok with rice, **plus** extra kangkong/vegetables from the soup; water before the meal
- **Evening:** Water or herbal tea; light snack only if truly hungry (a piece of fruit, a small handful of nuts)

## Workouts — Week 1 (3 sessions: e.g., Mon / Wed / Fri)

Each session: **5-min warm-up → 15-min main set → 5-min cool-down.** Rest 30–60 seconds between exercises. If an exercise hurts (not just "hard" — *hurts*), skip it.

### Warm-up (5 min, every session)
- March in place — 1 min
- Arm circles (forward + backward) — 30 sec each
- Leg swings (hold a wall/chair) — 30 sec each leg
- Torso twists — 30 sec
- Bodyweight squats (slow, shallow) — 1 min

### Session A — Monday
1. **Wall push-ups** — 3 sets × 10 reps *(stand arm's length from a wall, hands flat, bend elbows)*
2. **Chair-assisted squats** — 3 sets × 10 reps *(stand in front of a sturdy chair, sit back until you lightly touch, stand up)*
3. **Standing knee raises** — 3 sets × 10 per leg *(lift knee to hip height, hold 1 sec)*
4. **Dead bugs** — 3 sets × 8 per side *(lie on back, opposite arm + leg extend slowly)*

### Session B — Wednesday
1. **Incline push-ups** (hands on sturdy table/counter) — 3 sets × 8 reps
2. **Glute bridges** — 3 sets × 12 reps *(lie on back, knees bent, lift hips, squeeze 2 sec)*
3. **Reverse lunges** (hold wall for balance) — 3 sets × 8 per leg
4. **Bird dogs** — 3 sets × 8 per side *(on all fours, extend opposite arm + leg)*

### Session C — Friday
Repeat **Session A**, aiming for slightly better form than Monday. That's progress.

### Cool-down (5 min, every session)
- Standing quad stretch — 30 sec per leg (hold a wall)
- Hamstring stretch (heel on low step, lean forward) — 30 sec per leg
- Chest doorway stretch — 30 sec
- Child's pose — 1 min
- Slow deep breathing — 1 min

# WEEK 2 — Awareness: Know Your Numbers 🔍

**Rule for week 2:** Keep everything from week 1, and add *awareness* — no new restrictions.

## Daily Targets (every day, week 2)

| Target | Goal |
|---|---|
| 💧 Water | 2–2.5 litres |
| 🥗 Vegetables | 1+ serving at lunch AND dinner |
| 🍗 Protein | A palm-sized portion at every meal |
| 😴 Sleep | 7–8 hours |
| 🚶 Steps | **7,000 steps/day** (new!) |
| 🥤 Sugary drinks | None — water, unsweetened tea/coffee only |

## Nutrition Framework — Week 2

### The Hand-Portion Method (no scale needed)
- **Palm** (thickness + size of your palm) = protein portion → chicken, fish, pork, tofu, eggs
- **Fist** = vegetable portion
- **Cupped hand** = carb portion → rice, noodles, bread, kamote
- **Thumb** = fat portion → oil, butter, nuts, cheese

Aim per meal: **1–2 palms protein + 1–2 fists veggies + 1 cupped hand carbs + 1 thumb fat.**

### This week's nutrition jobs:
1. **Protein at every meal.** Breakfast is where most people miss — add 2 eggs, or leftover ulam, or Greek yogurt to whatever you already eat.
2. **Cut liquid calories.** One soda = ~140 calories with zero fullness. Switch to water, sparkling water with calamansi, or unsweetened tea. (Your week-1 log will show you exactly how much this was costing you.)
3. **Keep logging.** Compare week 2 to week 1 — notice patterns, not problems.

### Sample Day of Eating (Filipino-friendly, week 2 style)

- **Breakfast:** 2 boiled eggs + garlic fried rice (1 cupped hand) + sliced tomatoes; water first
- **Lunch:** Grilled liempo or chicken (1–2 palms) + 1 cup rice (1 cupped hand) + pinakbet (2 fists); water first
- **Snack:** Banana or a small handful of peanuts (1 thumb)
- **Dinner:** Paksiw na isda (1–2 palms) + 1 cup rice + steamed sitaw/kalabasa (2 fists); water first
- **Drinks all day:** Water, black coffee, or unsweetened salabat/tea

## Workouts — Week 2 (3 sessions: Mon / Wed / Fri)

Same structure (5-min warm-up → main set → 5-min cool-down), slightly more volume. Add **one extra set** to each exercise vs. week 1 (so 4 sets instead of 3), OR add 2 reps per set — your choice.

### Session A — Monday (repeat week-1 Session A + progression)
1. Wall push-ups — 3 × 12 *(or 4 × 10)*
2. Chair-assisted squats — 3 × 12
3. Standing knee raises — 3 × 12 per leg
4. Dead bugs — 3 × 10 per side

### Session B — Wednesday (repeat week-1 Session B + progression)
1. Incline push-ups — 3 × 10
2. Glute bridges — 3 × 15 *(add a 3-sec hold at the top)*
3. Reverse lunges — 3 × 10 per leg
4. Bird dogs — 3 × 10 per side *(hold 2 sec at extension)*

### Session C — Friday
Your choice: repeat whichever session felt better, with the same progression. End the week strong.

## Steps — The 7,000 Target
- Check your phone's built-in step counter for a baseline (most people are at 3,000–5,000).
- Bridge the gap with a **15–20 minute walk** — after lunch is ideal for digestion and blood sugar.
- Walking counts double: it burns calories *and* clears your head.

## Week-2 Check-In Questions

*Answer honestly — this is your coaching check-in. Write answers in your tracker.*

1. **Weight & measurements:** What changed since week 1? (Any direction is data, not a grade.)
2. **Protein:** How many days did you hit protein at every meal? What made it hard on the days you missed?
3. **Sugary drinks:** Did you eliminate them? If not, which one is hardest to give up — and what's your plan for it?
4. **Workouts:** Did you complete all 3 sessions both weeks? Which exercise feels strongest? Which needs work?
5. **Energy & sleep:** Rate your energy 1–10 vs. two weeks ago. What's one sleep habit you could improve?
6. **The log:** Looking at two weeks of food logs, what's the ONE pattern you'd most like to change in week 3?
7. **Confidence check:** On a scale of 1–10, how confident are you that you can keep this up for 6 more weeks? If below 7, what would raise it?

*Bring these answers into week 3 — they shape your protein targets and workout progression.*

## FAQ

**How much weight can I expect to lose?**
A safe, sustainable rate is roughly 0.5–1 kg (1–2 lbs) per week — so 4–8 kg (8–16 lbs) over 8 weeks is a realistic range for many people. Results vary a lot based on starting point, consistency, sleep, and stress. We focus on building habits that keep weight off long-term, not on chasing a number on the scale by week 8.

**Do I need a gym?**
No. The entire workout program uses bodyweight exercises you can do at home with zero equipment. All you need is a bit of floor space and comfortable clothes. If you *do* have gym access, the principles still apply — but it's never required.

**What if I have dietary restrictions?**
The nutrition framework is built around targets (protein, vegetables, water, portions) rather than specific foods, so it adapts to almost any restriction — halal, vegetarian, lactose-free, allergies, diabetes-friendly eating. During your week-2 check-in, you'll tailor the framework to your needs. If you have a medical condition affecting your diet, work with your doctor or a registered dietitian alongside this program.

**What happens after 8 weeks?**
Week 8 is all about maintenance — you'll have built the habits, learned your body's patterns, and created a routine you can sustain. The tracker sheets and frameworks are yours to keep using. Many graduates repeat weeks 5–8 as a maintenance cycle or restart the program at a higher intensity.

## ⚠️ Health Disclaimer

This program provides general health and fitness information for educational purposes only — **it is not medical advice**. Consult your doctor before starting any weight loss or exercise program, especially if you are pregnant, nursing, managing a medical condition (such as diabetes, heart disease, high blood pressure, or joint issues), taking medication, or have a history of disordered eating.

Results vary from person to person. **Stop exercising and seek medical attention if you experience pain, dizziness, chest discomfort, shortness of breath, or nausea.** Listen to your body — no workout is worth an injury.

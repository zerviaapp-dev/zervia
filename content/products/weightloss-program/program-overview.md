# The 8-Week Reset — Program Overview

*All 8 weeks at a glance. Full detail for weeks 1–2 is in `weeks-1-2.md`.*

---

## Week 1 — Foundations: Build the Base 🧱
- **Theme:** Set up systems, not restrictions. No dieting yet — just observation and small wins.
- **Nutrition focus:** Track what you currently eat (no judgment); add one vegetable to lunch and dinner; hit a daily water target (2–2.5 L).
- **Exercise focus:** Learn the movement basics — 3 gentle full-body bodyweight sessions (20–25 min). Focus on form, not intensity.
- **Milestone:** Baseline weigh-in + measurements recorded; water habit established; 3 workouts completed.

## Week 2 — Awareness: Know Your Numbers 🔍
- **Theme:** Calorie and portion awareness — understanding *how much* you're eating without obsessing.
- **Nutrition focus:** Learn hand-portion sizing (palm = protein, fist = veggies, cupped hand = carbs, thumb = fats); protein target at every meal; cut liquid calories (soda, sugary coffee, juice).
- **Exercise focus:** Same 3 sessions, slightly more volume; introduce the daily step target (7,000 steps).
- **Milestone:** Week-2 check-in questions answered; portion method in use; sugary drinks reduced or eliminated.

## Week 3 — Protein & Power 🍗
- **Theme:** Protein becomes the anchor of every meal — the most important nutrition lever for fat loss.
- **Nutrition focus:** Hit a daily protein target (~1.6 g per kg of bodyweight); protein-first meal building; Filipino-friendly high-protein swaps.
- **Exercise focus:** Add a fourth movement day OR increase session length to 30 min; introduce basic progression (more reps, slower tempo).
- **Milestone:** Protein target hit 5+ days; workouts feel easier than week 1.

## Week 4 — Strength Introduction 🏋️
- **Theme:** Build muscle to burn more at rest — strength training becomes a core pillar.
- **Nutrition focus:** Pre- and post-workout fueling; keep protein high; introduce the "plate method" (½ veg, ¼ protein, ¼ carbs) for every dinner.
- **Exercise focus:** Split-style sessions begin (upper / lower / full-body rotation); learn progressive overload simply (add 1–2 reps each week).
- **Milestone:** Mid-program check-in (week 4): compare measurements and photos to week 1; adjust targets.

## Week 5 — Energy & Engine 🔥
- **Theme:** Boost daily activity — fat loss accelerates when *all-day* movement rises, not just workouts.
- **Nutrition focus:** Fine-tune portions based on 4 weeks of data; address snacking triggers; meal-prep one staple (e.g., a big batch of protein + rice).
- **Exercise focus:** Add one "active day" — a 30–45 min walk, bike ride, swim, or dance session; step target rises to 8,000–10,000.
- **Milestone:** NEAT (non-exercise activity) noticeably higher; one full week of consistent tracking.

## Week 6 — Plateau Busters 🧗
- **Theme:** Plateaus are normal — learn to work through them instead of quitting.
- **Nutrition focus:** The "diet break" concept (2–3 days at maintenance calories to reset hunger hormones); re-audit portions — they've usually crept up.
- **Exercise focus:** Introduce interval-style finishers (20 sec work / 40 sec rest × 5) at the end of sessions; try one new movement variation.
- **Milestone:** Week-6 check-in: honest review of what's working; plateau strategy chosen and applied.

## Week 7 — Lifestyle Lock-In 🔒
- **Theme:** Make it automatic — shift from "program" thinking to "this is just how I live."
- **Nutrition focus:** Eating out and social events strategy (the 80/20 rule); build your 5 go-to meals you can cook without thinking.
- **Exercise focus:** Sessions stay consistent but flexible — learn the "minimum effective workout" (15 min) for busy days so you never skip entirely.
- **Milestone:** 5 go-to meals defined; never-miss-twice rule adopted; routine survives a busy week.

## Week 8 — Maintenance & Beyond 🌱
- **Theme:** Graduate — transition from fat loss to sustainable maintenance.
- **Nutrition focus:** Find your maintenance calories; reverse-diet basics (add ~100–150 kcal/week until weight stabilizes); keep protein and veggie habits forever.
- **Exercise focus:** Design your ongoing routine (3–4 sessions/week template); set a 3-month fitness goal beyond weight (strength, stamina, a sport).
- **Milestone:** Final check-in: full 8-week comparison (weight, measurements, photos, habits); maintenance plan written; celebrate how far you've come.

---

## Progression Logic

| Phase | Weeks | Goal |
|---|---|---|
| Build | 1–2 | Habits, awareness, movement basics |
| Fuel | 3–4 | Protein mastery, strength introduction |
| Push | 5–6 | Activity volume, plateau strategies |
| Sustain | 7–8 | Lifestyle integration, maintenance transition |

*Each phase builds on the last. Don't skip ahead — the early "easy" weeks are what make the later weeks stick.*

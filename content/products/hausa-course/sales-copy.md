Planning a trip to northern Nigeria or Niger? Working with Hausa-speaking colleagues? Married into a Hausa family? Or simply drawn to one of Africa's great languages?

## What makes this course different

**Conversational from the very first lesson.** Every module is built around real situations — the market, the taxi rank, the family visit, the office — so everything you learn is something you'll actually say.

**Pronunciation help that actually helps.** Hausa has a few sounds English doesn't — the famous ƙ, ɗ, ɓ, ƴ. We break each one down with plain-English mouth guides and drills, so you sound like yourself trying, not like a phrasebook.

**Culture woven in, not bolted on.** In Hausa life, the greeting *is* the relationship. You'll learn the elaborate greeting rituals, the Islamic customs behind daily rhythms, when to use your right hand, and how to greet elders and women respectfully — the notes that turn "correct words" into genuine connection.

**Real-life scenarios, start to finish.** Each module ends with the situations you'll actually face: negotiating a taxi fare before you get in, declining food politely at a family home, finding your way when you're lost. No filler vocabulary you'll never use.

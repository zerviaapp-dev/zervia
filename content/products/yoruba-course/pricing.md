# Yoruba Course — Pricing

## Tiers

| Tier | Price | What you get |
|---|---|---|
| **Single course** | **$39** | Full *Learn Yoruba* ecourse — 10 modules, 58 lessons, lifetime access |
| **3-course bundle** | **$89** | Yoruba + any 2 other Zervia language courses — save $28 vs. buying separately |
| **All 9 languages** | **$199** | Every Zervia language ecourse, lifetime access — save $152 vs. buying separately |

## Rationale

- **$39 base** sits in the sweet spot of the $29–49 impulse range: low enough for a curious learner to say yes, high enough to signal a serious, complete course rather than a pamphlet.
- **$89 bundle** rewards the polyglot impulse (many language learners study 2–3 languages) and lifts average order value by ~2.3× while still feeling like a deal.
- **$199 all-languages** captures the committed learner and gift buyers; at ~$22 per language it undercuts every competitor's per-course price while maximizing revenue per customer.
- Single-price simplicity at checkout: no subscriptions, no upsell maze — one honest price per tier, lifetime access.

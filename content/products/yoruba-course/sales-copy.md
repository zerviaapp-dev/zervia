Yoruba is spoken by more than 45 million people across Nigeria, Benin, Togo, and a thriving global diaspora. It's the language of Lagos markets, Nollywood films, Afrobeat lyrics — and of grandparents whose stories deserve to be heard in their own words.

This course teaches you to **actually hold conversations** — not to recite grammar tables.

## What you'll be able to do

- **Greet anyone, any time of day** — with the right greeting for morning, afternoon, and evening, and the respect forms Yoruba culture expects
- **Get the tones right** — Yoruba is a tonal language, and this course treats pronunciation as a first-class skill from lesson one, with tone marks on every new word
- **Handle real life** — ordering food, bargaining at the market, taking a taxi, checking into a hotel, seeing a doctor
- **Understand the culture behind the words** — why elders receive special greetings, what a proverb really means, how respect is shown and earned
- **Talk about your world** — family, work, travel, celebrations — in natural, everyday Yoruba

## What's inside

- **10 modules, 58 lessons** — from your first sounds to full conversation practice
- **Tone-marked vocabulary throughout** — high (á), mid (a), low (à) marks on every new word so you learn pronunciation correctly from day one
- **Cultural notes in every module** — proverbs (owé), etiquette, and the "why" behind the customs
- **Real-life scenarios** — mini-dialogues for the market, the taxi rank, the restaurant, the family visit
- **Practice in every lesson** — short exercises that get you speaking, not just reading
- **Lifetime access** — learn at your own pace, revisit anytime

# Learn Yoruba — From Beginner to Conversational

**Full curriculum outline** · Taught in English for English speakers · Conversational focus

---

## Module 1 — Sounds, Tones & the Yoruba Alphabet
- **1.1 The Yoruba alphabet** — the 25 letters, special characters (ẹ, ọ, ṣ), and what's missing vs. English
- **1.2 The three tones** — high (á), mid (a), low (à): why tone changes meaning (ìgbà vs. igbá)
- **1.3 Tone drills** — minimal pairs practice so your ear catches the difference
- **1.4 Vowels & nasal sounds** — getting ẹ, ọ, and nasal vowels right
- **1.5 Pronunciation of everyday words** — slow-speed and natural-speed audio-style breakdowns
- **1.6 Common learner mistakes** — the tone slips English speakers make (and how to fix them)

## Module 2 — Greetings & Introductions
- **2.1 Time-of-day greetings** — Ẹ káàárọ̀, Ẹ káàsán, Ẹ káalẹ́ and when each applies
- **2.2 Ẹ vs. O: the respect system** — plural "you" for elders, singular for peers
- **2.3 Báwo ni? and answers** — how-are-you exchanges beyond the textbook
- **2.4 Introducing yourself** — Orúkọ mi ni…, Níbo ni o ti wá?, mo ní… ọdún
- **2.5 Ẹ ṣeun & Ẹ káàbọ̀** — thank-you and welcome, done the Yoruba way
- **2.6 Saying goodbye** — Ó dàa, Ó di ìgbà mìíràn, and parting politely

## Module 3 — Numbers, Time & Dates
- **3.1 Numbers 1–20** — with the special logic of the Yoruba vigesimal system
- **3.2 Tens to 100 and beyond** — ọgbọ̀n, ogójì, ọgọ́ta… counting in chunks of 20
- **3.3 Telling the time** — àago méje, ìṣẹ́jú, and Yoruba clock expressions
- **3.4 Days of the week** — Ọjọ́ Ajé through Ọjọ́ Àìkú, plus weekend phrases
- **3.5 Months, seasons & dates** — giving dates and asking "when?"
- **3.6 Ages, prices & quantities** — numbers in real sentences

## Module 4 — Food & Dining
- **4.1 At the table** — mo fẹ́…, staple dishes: ẹ̀bà, ìyán, àmàlà, ẹ̀gúsí
- **4.2 Ordering in a restaurant** — polite requests, asking what's available
- **4.3 Street food & snacks** — àkàrà, mọ́yínmọ́yín, dòdò, suya vocabulary
- **4.4 Dietary needs & preferences** — "no pepper," "vegetarian," allergies
- **4.5 Complimenting the cook & table manners** — ẹ ṣeun and cultural do's
- **4.6 Cooking verbs at home** — sè (cook), dín (fry), and kitchen basics

## Module 5 — Shopping & Money at the Market
- **5.1 Ẹló ni?** — asking prices and understanding the answer
- **5.2 Naira & kobo** — money words, counting cash, mobile payment phrases
- **5.3 Bargaining politely** — Ṣé o lè dín kù? and the rhythm of haggling
- **5.4 Clothes, colors & sizes** — aṣọ vocabulary for the tailor and the market
- **5.5 "Just looking" and walking away** — graceful exits that keep the door open
- **5.6 Receipts, change & problems** — confirming what you paid

## Module 6 — Travel & Directions
- **6.1 Níbo ni… wà?** — asking where things are
- **6.2 Taxis, danfos & okadas** — negotiating fares and giving destinations
- **6.3 Directions vocabulary** — right, left, straight, near, far, behind
- **6.4 The airport & bus park** — tickets, delays, luggage phrases
- **6.5 Hotels & lodging** — check-in, asking for what you need
- **6.6 Getting lost gracefully** — asking strangers for help without panic

## Module 7 — Family, Friends & Social Life
- **7.1 Family vocabulary** — bàbá, ìyá, ọmọ, arákùnrin, arábìnrin, grandparents
- **7.2 Proverbs in conversation** — owé Yorùbá and when people actually use them
- **7.3 Celebrations** — weddings, naming ceremonies, birthdays: what to say
- **7.4 Condolences & sympathy** — respectful words in hard moments
- **7.5 Making friends** — small talk, compliments, keeping in touch
- **7.6 Visiting someone's home** — arrival greetings, gifts, and good manners

## Module 8 — Work & Business Phrases
- **8.1 Office introductions** — titles, departments, "I work as…"
- **8.2 Meetings & appointments** — scheduling, confirming, rescheduling
- **8.3 Phone & email basics** — greetings, "can I speak to…", leaving messages
- **8.4 Working with colleagues** — requests, thanks, teamwork phrases
- **8.5 Negotiating & agreements** — clear, polite business language
- **8.6 After-work socializing** — drinks, invitations, saying thanks

## Module 9 — Emergencies & Health
- **9.1 Ẹ ràn mí lọ́wọ́!** — asking for help urgently
- **9.2 At the clinic** — Mo nílò dókítà, describing symptoms and pain
- **9.3 Pharmacy phrases** — asking for medicine, dosage questions
- **9.4 Police & safety** — reporting problems, asking for directions to safety
- **9.5 Allergies & medical history** — critical phrases for your health
- **9.6 Insurance & paperwork** — practical words for forms and offices

## Module 10 — Culture, Etiquette & Conversation Practice
- **10.1 Respect for elders** — kneeling (kúnlẹ̀) and prostrating (dọ̀bálẹ̀) greetings explained
- **10.2 Greeting as a way of life** — occupational greetings (kú iṣẹ́) and situational greetings
- **10.3 Taboos & polite behavior** — what not to do, and why
- **10.4 Storytelling & humor** — jokes, idioms, and sounding natural
- **10.5 Conversation lab 1** — guided role-plays: market, taxi, restaurant
- **10.6 Conversation lab 2** — longer dialogues + self-assessment checklist and next steps

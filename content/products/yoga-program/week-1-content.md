# Yoga for Real Life — Week 1: Foundations & Breath (FULL CONTENT)

**Session rhythm:** 5-minute warm-up → 20-minute main practice → 5-minute cool-down & Savasana.

Instructor notes: Speak slowly and warmly. Demo every pose before asking the class to join. Always offer the modification first — beginners follow the easier option without shame when it's presented as the default.

---

# Session 1 — "Hello, Mat" (Set-Up Basics)

## Welcome (2 min)

"Welcome to Yoga for Real Life. I'm so glad you're here. Today is Day 1 — and Day 1 is the bravest day, so give yourself credit. This program assumes you've never done yoga. We're going to start with the two things every pose is built on: standing well and breathing well. Keep a chair or wall nearby if you like having something to hold. There's no wrong way to do today — there is only your way."

## Warm-up (5 min)

**1. Seated or Standing Belly Breath — 8 breaths**
Sit tall or stand with feet hip-width apart. Place one hand on your belly, one on your chest.
- Cue: "Breathe in through your nose for 4 counts — feel your belly rise like a balloon."
- Cue: "Breathe out slowly through your nose for 6 counts — feel the belly soften back."
- "Let the shoulders stay heavy. No effort in the neck."
- *Modification:* If counting feels stressful, just breathe slowly and notice the belly moving.

**2. Gentle Neck Rolls — 1 min**
Drop your chin toward your chest. Slowly roll the right ear toward the right shoulder, then back through centre to the left. 3 slow circles each direction.
- Cue: "Move like you're in slow motion. If you find a tight spot, pause and breathe there."
- *Modification:* Skip the full circle — just do gentle side-to-side tilts.

**3. Shoulder Rolls — 1 min**
Roll both shoulders up toward your ears, back, and down. 6 slow rolls, then reverse.
- Cue: "Imagine you're drawing big lazy circles with your shoulder blades. Squeeze them gently together at the back."

**4. Standing Side Stretch — 30 sec each side**
Feet hip-width apart. Interlace fingers, press palms to the sky, lean gently to the right.
- Cue: "Inhale to grow tall; exhale to lean. Feel the long line from hip to fingertips."
- *Modification:* Keep hands on hips and just lean — same stretch, less arm work.

## Main Practice (20 min)

**5. Mountain Pose (Tadasana) — 5 breaths**
Stand with feet hip-width apart, toes pointing forward. Arms relaxed at your sides, palms facing forward.
- Cue: "Press all four corners of each foot into the mat. Lift your kneecaps gently. Tuck the tailbone slightly. Lengthen the back of your neck — chin parallel to the floor."
- Cue: "This is the mother of all standing poses. Every time you return here, it's home base."
- *Modification:* Feet wider for balance; stand near a wall.

**6. Arms Overhead Stretch — 5 breaths**
From Mountain, inhale arms up overhead.
- Cue: "Reach the fingertips to the ceiling, but keep the shoulders away from the ears. Exhale, soften the ribs down."

**7. Forward Fold (Uttanasana) with bent knees — 5 breaths**
Exhale, hinge at the hips and fold forward. Bend the knees generously; let the head hang.
- Cue: "Think of your torso as heavy. Nod your head yes and no — let the neck completely relax."
- *Modification:* Hands on blocks, books, or thighs instead of reaching the floor. Bend knees as much as you like.

**8. Half Lift (Ardha Uttanasana) — 3 rounds**
Inhale, lift the torso halfway — flat back, hands on shins or thighs, gaze slightly forward.
- Cue: "Lengthen from tailbone to crown, like a table top. Exhale, fold back down."
- "Move between fold and half lift with your breath — three slow rounds."

**9. Wide-Legged Standing Forward Fold (Prasarita) — 5 breaths**
Step feet wide apart (about one leg length). Fold forward, hands to blocks or the floor.
- Cue: "Let gravity do the work. Heavy head, soft knees."

**10. Chair-Assisted Warrior II Preview — 5 breaths each side**
Place a chair to your right. Step the right foot forward into a small lunge, bend the front knee slightly, turn the left foot out 45°. Hold the chair, extend the left arm forward.
- Cue: "This is a sneak peek of Week 2! Front knee tracks over the ankle. Chest open, gaze forward."
- *Modification:* Tiny bend in the knee is plenty — we're learning the shape, not holding a deep pose.

**11. Standing Figure-4 Hip Opener — 5 breaths each side**
Hold the chair. Cross the right ankle over the left knee, sit back slightly as if into a chair.
- Cue: "You should feel a stretch deep in the right outer hip. If you feel the knee, come out a little."
- *Modification:* Do it lying on your back — cross the ankle over the knee and hug the bottom leg in.

**12. Mountain Pose return — 5 breaths**
Return to Mountain. Close the eyes for the last 2 breaths.
- Cue: "Notice how you feel compared to the start of class. A little taller? A little calmer? That's yoga working."

## Cool-down & Savasana (5 min)

**Seated rest — 1 min**
Sit on the mat (or a chair). Hands on knees. Take 5 slow breaths.

**Supine Savasana — 4 min**
Lie on your back. Legs extended, arms at your sides, palms up. Cover yourself with a blanket if you like.
- Cue: "Let the whole body be heavy. The work is done — this is the most important pose. Let the mat hold you."
- Guide 3 slow breaths, then let them rest in silence for 2 minutes.
- "Wiggle your fingers and toes. Roll to your right side. Press up to seated. Hands at your heart — thank yourself for showing up. Namaste."

# Session 2 — "Root to Rise" (Grounding Poses)

## Welcome (1 min)

"Welcome back! Today we go down to the floor — gently. We'll learn Tabletop, the all-fours position that's home base for half of yoga, and a few poses that wake up your back, core, and balance. Keep your blocks nearby and a wall within reach."

## Warm-up (5 min)

**1. Seated Belly Breath — 8 breaths**
Same as Session 1. Remind them: "In for 4, out for 6. Belly rises, belly softens."

**2. Seated Cat–Cow — 8 rounds**
Sit tall. Inhale: arch the back gently, lift the chest, look slightly up. Exhale: round the back, tuck the chin, draw the belly in.
- Cue: "Let the breath move the spine. Inhale opens the front body; exhale rounds the back body."

**3. Seated Side Stretch — 30 sec each side**
Same as Session 1.

**4. Gentle Seated Twist — 5 breaths each side**
Sit tall. Right hand to left knee, left hand behind you. Inhale to lengthen; exhale to twist gently.
- Cue: "Twist from the belly, not the neck. Every inhale, grow taller; every exhale, turn a fraction more."

## Main Practice (20 min)

**5. Tabletop (Bharmanasana) — 5 breaths**
Come to all fours: hands under shoulders, knees under hips. Spread the fingers wide.
- Cue: "Press the floor away. Belly gently drawn in — not sucked in, just awake. Back long like a table."
- *Modification:* Folded blanket under the knees for cushioning.

**6. Cat–Cow on all fours — 8 rounds**
Inhale: drop the belly, lift the tailbone and chest (Cow). Exhale: round the spine, tuck chin and tailbone (Cat).
- Cue: "Move one vertebra at a time. This is a massage for your spine."

**7. Bird Dog — 5 breaths each side**
From Tabletop, extend the right arm forward and left leg back. Hold, breathing steadily. Switch sides.
- Cue: "Imagine a glass of water balanced on your lower back — keep it still. Gaze down at the mat."
- *Modification:* Just the leg, or just the arm. Touch the wall with the extended foot for balance feedback.

**8. Downward Dog with bent knees (Adho Mukha Svanasana) — 5 breaths, then pedal 30 sec**
From Tabletop, tuck the toes, lift the hips up and back into an inverted V. Keep knees generously bent, heels lifted.
- Cue: "Push the floor away with your hands. Hips high, spine long. Pedal the feet — bend one knee, then the other, like you're walking in place."
- *Modification:* Hands on a chair seat instead of the floor (a much kinder angle for wrists and hamstrings).

**9. Child's Pose (Balasana) — 8 breaths**
Knees wide, big toes touching, sit the hips back toward the heels, forehead to the mat, arms extended or alongside the body.
- Cue: "This is your rest pose — come here any time in any class, no permission needed. Breathe into your back; feel it expand."

**10. Low Lunge with back knee down — 5 breaths each side**
From Tabletop, step the right foot forward between the hands. Lower the left knee to the mat (blanket under it). Hands on blocks or the front thigh. Sink gently.
- Cue: "Tuck the tailbone slightly — you'll feel the stretch move into the front of the left hip. Chest lifted, shoulders soft."
- *Modification:* Hands stay on blocks; keep the torso more upright for less intensity.

**11. Bridge Pose (Setu Bandha) — 5 breaths, 2 rounds**
Lie on your back, knees bent, feet flat and hip-width apart, arms at your sides. Press through the feet to lift the hips.
- Cue: "Squeeze the glutes at the top. Keep the knees tracking over the ankles — imagine holding a block between the thighs."
- *Modification:* Smaller lift, or rest the hips on a block (supported bridge — pure relaxation).

**12. Supine Twist — 5 breaths each side**
Hug both knees to the chest, then drop them to the right, arms in a T. Gaze left (or keep looking up).
- Cue: "Let gravity do the twisting. Every exhale, soften a little deeper."

## Cool-down & Savasana (5 min)

**Legs resting on a chair — 2 min**
Lie on your back with calves resting on a chair seat. "The laziest, loveliest rest. Let the lower back melt into the floor."

**Savasana — 3 min**
Legs extended, arms at sides. Same closing ritual as Session 1: 3 guided breaths, 90 seconds of silence, roll to the side, press up, hands at heart. "Thank yourself for Day 2."

# Session 3 — "Breathe & Bend" (First Full Sequence)

## Welcome (1 min)

"Day 3 of Week 1 — you're building a real habit. Today we connect everything you've learned into your first flowing sequence. It will feel simple, and that's the point: simple done well is powerful. Let's move."

## Warm-up (5 min)

**1. Sun Breaths — 6 rounds**
Stand in Mountain. Inhale: sweep the arms up overhead. Exhale: float them back down.
- Cue: "Arms rise with the inhale like the sun coming up; fall with the exhale like the sun setting. Let the breath lead — the arms follow."

**2. Shoulder rolls + neck rolls — 2 min**
As in Session 1.

**3. Gentle marching in place — 1 min**
Lift knees alternately, swinging the arms naturally.
- Cue: "Wake up the legs and get the blood moving. Smile — it actually helps."

## Main Practice (20 min)

**The Week 1 Flow — repeat 4 rounds, moving with the breath:**

**4. Mountain Pose — 2 breaths**
"Arrive. Feet grounded, spine tall."

**5. Arms Overhead + Side Stretch — inhale up, exhale lean right, inhale centre, exhale lean left**
- Cue: "Inhale to grow; exhale to lean. Keep both feet pressing down."

**6. Forward Fold (knees bent) — exhale, 3 breaths**
- Cue: "Heavy torso, relaxed neck. Nod yes, shake no."

**7. Half Lift — inhale, hold 2 breaths**
- Cue: "Flat back, long spine. Hands on shins."

**8. Forward Fold — exhale, 2 breaths, then roll up to Mountain**
- Cue: "Bend the knees, tuck the chin, and roll up one vertebra at a time. Head comes up last."

**Between rounds:** 3 Sun Breaths to reset.

**After 4 rounds, add:**

**9. Warrior II Preview (both sides) — 5 breaths each**
As in Session 1, chair optional.

**10. Standing Figure-4 (both sides) — 5 breaths each**
As in Session 1.

**11. Seated Twist (both sides) — 5 breaths each**
As in Session 2.

**12. Supine Figure-4 Hip Stretch — 5 breaths each side**
Lie on your back. Cross the right ankle over the left knee, hug the left thigh toward you.
- Cue: "Flex the right foot to protect the knee. Breathe into the outer hip — this is where desk-sitting lives."

## Cool-down & Savasana (5 min)

**Guided Savasana — 5 min**
Lie back. "This is your longest rest yet — you've earned it."
- Minute 1: "Scan from your feet to your head. Wherever you find tension, breathe into it and let it soften."
- Minute 2–3: Silence. "Just breathe. Nothing to do, nowhere to be."
- Minute 4: "Begin to deepen the breath. Wiggle fingers and toes."
- Minute 5: "Roll to your right side. Rest there a moment. Press up to seated."
- Closing: "Place your hands at your heart. Three weeks from now, you'll flow through a full practice — and it starts with what you did today. Thank yourself. Namaste."

**Week 1 complete!** 🎉 Remind them: "If Week 1 felt easy, wonderful — the strength work of Week 2 is waiting. If it felt challenging, repeat Week 1. There's no behind here."

## FAQ

**I've never done yoga. Will I be lost?**
Not at all. Week 1 is called Foundations & Breath for a reason — every pose is introduced from scratch, with what to do, how to breathe, and where you should feel it. You'll never be expected to know anything in advance.

**I have bad knees / a bad back. Can I still do this?**
Almost certainly yes. Every pose comes with a modification — bent-knee options, chair-supported standing poses, and blocks to bring the floor closer. That said, if you have a medical condition, check with your doctor first, and always move in a pain-free range. Discomfort from stretching is fine; sharp pain is a stop sign.

**How much space do I need?**
Just enough to lie down on your mat with your arms stretched wide — roughly 2 × 2 metres. A corner of a living room or bedroom works perfectly.

**What if I miss a week?**
Nothing breaks. The program is self-paced with lifetime access. Missed a week? Just pick up where you left off — Week 1 is always there if you want a refresher before moving on. Consistency beats perfection: two sessions a week is still a win.

# Yoga for Real Life — Program Overview

**4 weeks · 12 sessions · 30 minutes each · 3 sessions per week**

Each session follows the same friendly rhythm: a 5-minute warm-up, a 20-minute main practice, and a 5-minute cool-down with Savasana. Sessions build week by week, so by Week 4 you're flowing through a complete mindful practice.

---

## Week 1 — Foundations & Breath

**Theme:** Meet your body. Learn how to breathe, stand, and move with awareness.

- **Session 1 — "Hello, Mat" (Set-Up Basics):** Welcome and intention-setting. Breathing basics (belly breath and slow counting). Foundational standing poses introduced one by one: Mountain Pose, gentle Side Stretch, Forward Fold with bent knees, and a wide-legged resting pose. Lots of standing, very little getting up and down. End with a short guided relaxation.
- **Session 2 — "Root to Rise" (Grounding Poses):** Warm-up with Cat–Cow and shoulder rolls. Learn Tabletop position and move through Bird Dog (core + balance), Downward Dog with bent knees and heel lifts, and a gentle Child's Pose rest sequence. Finish with Legs-Up-the-Wall-style rest and Savasana.
- **Session 3 — "Breathe & Bend" (First Full Sequence):** Sun-breath warm-up (arms rising and falling with the breath). Chain the Week 1 poses into a slow standing sequence: Mountain → Side Stretch → Forward Fold → Half Lift → Mountain. Add a seated twist and a supine figure-4 hip stretch. Close with a 3-minute guided Savasana.

**Week 1 goal:** Know 8 foundational poses, breathe deeply on cue, and feel comfortable on the mat.

---

## Week 2 — Building Strength

**Theme:** Wake up your muscles. Legs, core, and arms — gently, and without strain.

- **Session 1 — "Strong Legs" (Warrior Foundations):** Warm-up with joint circles and marching in place. Introduce Warrior I and Warrior II with generous stance and bent front knee, plus Triangle Pose with a block. Hold each 5–8 breaths. Wall option for balance. Cool-down with a standing forward fold and seated rest.
- **Session 2 — "Core Kindness" (Belly & Back):** Tabletop warm-up with Cat–Cow. Boat Pose with feet down or shins parallel (your choice), slow knee-to-chest crunches, Bridge Pose for glutes and back, and Locust Pose (mini backbend) for the spine. Finish with a gentle supine twist and Savasana.
- **Session 3 — "Steady Arms" (Upper Body & Plank):** Shoulder warm-up and wall push-ups. Knee-down Plank building toward full Plank, Downward Dog pedalling, and a gentle Chaturanga-on-knees option. End with Child's Pose, a chest-opening reclined stretch, and guided rest.

**Week 2 goal:** Hold Warrior II for 8 breaths, plank for 30 seconds (knees down counts!), and feel steady through a full standing sequence.

---

## Week 3 — Flexibility & Balance

**Theme:** Open the hips and hamstrings, find your balance, and discover how good "loose" feels.

- **Session 1 — "Open Hips" (Hip Mobility):** Warm-up with hip circles and gentle lunges. Low Lunge with back knee down, Lizard Pose option with blocks, Pigeon Pose (or figure-4 on your back as the kind alternative), and a wide-legged seated fold. Cool-down with supine twist and Savasana.
- **Session 2 — "Find Your Balance" (Standing Balance):** Warm-up with ankle circles and weight shifts. Tree Pose (foot on calf or ankle — never the knee), Warrior III with hands on a chair or wall, and Dancer Pose prep with a strap/towel. Every balance pose has a wall or chair option. End with seated forward fold and rest.
- **Session 3 — "Long & Loose" (Hamstrings & Spine):** Warm-up with slow Sun-breath flow. Standing forward fold with a strap, Half Splits (with blocks), seated Wide-Angle fold, and a gentle spinal twist series. Finish with the longest Savasana yet — 4 full minutes of guided rest.

**Week 3 goal:** Balance in Tree Pose for 5 breaths (wobbles welcome!), touch a block in Triangle with ease, and notice the difference in your hamstrings since Week 1.

---

## Week 4 — Flow & Mindfulness

**Theme:** Put it all together. Move with your breath through a complete, mindful practice.

- **Session 1 — "Gentle Flow I" (Sun Salutation Lite):** Warm-up with breath awareness. Learn a beginner Sun Salutation: Mountain → arms up → Forward Fold → Half Lift → step back to Plank (knees down OK) → lower → Cobra → Downward Dog → step forward → rise. Repeat 4 rounds, slow and steady. Cool-down with a seated twist and Savasana.
- **Session 2 — "Gentle Flow II" (Standing Flow):** Warm-up with Cat–Cow and shoulder rolls. Flow between Warrior I, Warrior II, Triangle, and Wide-Legged Forward Fold — one breath per movement, returning to Mountain between sides. Add Tree Pose as a balance "dessert." End with Legs-Up-the-Wall rest and guided Savasana.
- **Session 3 — "Your Practice" (Celebration Flow & Mindfulness):** A 5-minute seated breathing meditation to open. Then YOUR flow — a guided 20-minute practice that revisits favourite poses from all 4 weeks, with the instructor offering choices at each step so you learn to listen to your body. Close the program with an extended 5-minute Savasana and a short reflection on how far you've come.

**Week 4 goal:** Flow through a full Sun Salutation with your breath, choose your own pose options, and finish the program feeling strong, loose, and calm.

---

## After the program

Repeat any week you loved, string favourite sessions together, or simply press play on Week 4 Session 3 whenever you want a complete practice. You have lifetime access — this program grows with you. 💛

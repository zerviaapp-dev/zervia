## Module 2 — Mathematics Review

*The foundation everything else sits on. Roughly a third of FE questions touch math directly — and math fluency speeds up every other topic.*

1. **Algebra & Functions Refresher** — solving systems, quadratics, logarithms, exponentials, complex numbers, and the algebra mistakes that cost points under time pressure
2. **Calculus Essentials** — derivatives and integrals of common functions, partial derivatives, optimization, areas and volumes, differential equations basics (first-order and simple second-order)
3. **Statistics & Probability** — mean/median/mode, standard deviation, normal distribution, confidence intervals, linear regression, basic probability rules and expected value
4. **Linear Algebra Essentials** — matrices, determinants, solving linear systems, eigenvalues and eigenvectors at the level the FE actually tests
5. **Numerical Methods Quick Hits** — root-finding (bisection, Newton-Raphson), numerical integration (trapezoidal, Simpson's), interpolation — handbook-driven, not proof-driven
6. **Math Timed Drill Set** — 25 questions, 70 minutes, full solutions; the benchmark drill for "am I fast enough at math yet?"

**Formula cheat sheet:** derivative/integral tables, probability distributions, matrix identities, numerical method formulas.

## Module 3 — Statics & Dynamics Essentials

*The civil core. If your degree is in civil, this is home turf — the goal is speed and accuracy, not learning from scratch.*

1. **Force Systems & Equilibrium** — resultants, free-body diagrams, 2D/3D equilibrium equations, trusses (method of joints and sections), frames and machines
2. **Friction, Centroids & Moments of Inertia** — belt friction, wedge problems, centroid of composite areas, parallel-axis theorem, mass moments of inertia
3. **Kinematics of Particles & Rigid Bodies** — rectilinear and curvilinear motion, relative motion, rotating frames, instantaneous centers
4. **Kinetics: Force, Mass & Acceleration** — Newton's second law in all its forms, work-energy principle, impulse-momentum, impact and collisions
5. **Vibrations & Simple Systems** — free and forced vibration, natural frequency, damping basics — the small topic that shows up more than you'd expect
6. **Statics/Dynamics Timed Drill Set** — 30 questions, 85 minutes, full step-by-step solutions with free-body diagram walkthroughs

**Formula cheat sheet:** equilibrium equations, friction formulas, kinematic equations, work-energy and impulse-momentum forms, vibration equations.

---

## Module 4 — Mechanics of Materials & Fluid Mechanics Basics

*Two topics, one module — both are formula-and-handbook heavy, which means they're very trainable.*

1. **Stress, Strain & Axial Loading** — normal and shear stress, Hooke's law, axial deformation, thermal stress, stress concentrations, factor of safety
2. **Torsion, Bending & Shear** — torsion formula, angle of twist, flexure formula, shear stress in beams, shear and moment diagrams done fast
3. **Combined Loading & Failure Theories** — stress transformation, Mohr's circle (the 5-minute method), principal stresses, maximum shear stress theory, distortion energy theory
4. **Fluid Properties & Hydrostatics** — density, viscosity, pressure, manometers, hydrostatic forces on plane and curved surfaces, buoyancy
5. **Fluid Dynamics & Pipe Flow** — continuity, Bernoulli, energy equation, head loss (Darcy-Weisbach, Hazen-Williams), pump systems, open-channel flow basics (Manning's equation)
6. **Mech/Fluids Timed Drill Set** — 30 questions, 85 minutes, full solutions; heavy emphasis on handbook lookup speed

**Formula cheat sheet:** stress/strain formulas, Mohr's circle steps, beam tables, fluid property conversions, Bernoulli and head-loss forms, Manning's equation.

## Module 5 — Practice Question Sets

*This is where preparation becomes readiness. Every set is timed, every question has a full step-by-step solution.*

1. **Topic Drill: Mathematics** — 40 questions across algebra, calculus, statistics, and linear algebra; solutions show the fastest valid method, not just any method
2. **Topic Drill: Statics & Dynamics** — 40 questions; free-body diagram discipline drilled until it's automatic
3. **Topic Drill: Mechanics of Materials** — 35 questions; Mohr's circle and beam problems under the clock
4. **Topic Drill: Fluid Mechanics** — 35 questions; unit conversions and handbook lookups as a trained skill
5. **Mixed Breadth Mini-Exams (×2)** — 55 questions each, 2.5 hours each; mixed topics in exam order to practice triage and pacing
6. **Full-Length Simulated Exam** — 110 questions, 5 hours 20 minutes, exam-day rules; complete solutions plus a score-analysis worksheet that maps your misses to topics for targeted review

**Includes:** score-analysis worksheet, "miss log" template (record every wrong answer, the concept behind it, and the fix), and a retake targeting guide.

## Module 6 — Test-Day Playbook

*Knowledge gets you to the exam. This module gets you through it.*

1. **Time Management Under Pressure** — the 2.8-minute budget, the two-pass method, when to abandon a question, the 30-minute checkpoint system
2. **Question Triage Mastery** — classifying questions in 10 seconds (do now / do later / guess and move), spotting the "free points" the exam hides in plain sight
3. **FE Reference Handbook Navigation** — the handbook is searchable on test day; learn its structure, search keywords, and the 20 pages you'll visit most — this alone is worth 5–10 questions
4. **Educated Guessing, Done Right** — no penalty for wrong answers means never leaving a blank; elimination tactics, unit-check guessing, and the "reasonable magnitude" rule
5. **Mindset & Stamina** — managing the mid-exam slump (it hits everyone around question 60–70), breathing resets, what to do when panic arrives, the scheduled break strategy
6. **Test-Day Logistics Checklist** — ID requirements, what to bring, arrival timing, the break room plan, food and hydration, what happens if tech issues occur — zero surprises on the day

**Includes:** printable one-page test-day checklist, handbook "greatest hits" page map, and a pre-exam week wind-down schedule.

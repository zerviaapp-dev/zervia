## FAQ

**1. Will I flood my house?**
Honest answer: only if you skip the safety steps — and this course is designed so you can't. The very first thing you learn, before any repair lesson, is how to find and operate your main water shut-off and every fixture shut-off. Every repair walkthrough starts with "turn off the water and verify it's off." If you follow the steps in order, the worst case is a damp towel, not a flood. That said — water damage is real and expensive, which is why each module teaches you the clear signs that a job is beyond DIY and it's time to call a licensed plumber.

**2. Do I need any experience to take this course?**
None at all. Module 1 assumes you've never thought about your plumbing before. The lessons build from "how does water get to my tap" up to real repairs, and every technical term is explained in plain language the first time it appears.

**3. What tools will I need?**
A short, affordable list: a flange plunger, a cup plunger, an adjustable wrench, a hand drain auger, PTFE (Teflon) thread tape, a bucket, and towels. The included Tools Checklist gives you the complete kit with approximate costs — most people can assemble it for well under $100 CAD, and the tools pay for themselves the first time you skip a service call.

**4. When should I call a plumber instead of doing it myself?**
Clear answer: call a licensed plumber for anything involving the main water line, sewer line work, gas lines (never touch these), water heater internals, any job where local code requires a permit or licensed professional, and any situation where you're unsure and water is actively leaking. This course gives you a concrete "know your limits" framework in Module 1 and a "when to call a pro" checkpoint in every repair lesson — so it's always a clear decision, never a guess. When in doubt, call one: a $200 service call is always cheaper than a $5,000 water damage repair.

## Rationale

- **$39 base** sits comfortably inside the proven $29–49 impulse-buy band for self-paced ecourses. It's low enough that a curious learner doesn't agonize, and high enough to signal a serious, complete course rather than a pamphlet. It also positions cleanly against the bundle math below.
- **$89 for 3 courses** (≈ $29.67 per course) rewards the most common real buying pattern — learners rarely study one language in isolation (e.g., Igbo + Yoruba + Hausa, or Igbo plus a partner's language). The $28 saving is tangible without cheapening the single-course price.
- **$199 for all 9** (≈ $22 per course) targets the committed polyglot, the heritage learner collecting family languages, and gift buyers. The $152 headline saving makes it feel like a genuine collection, while the per-course floor stays high enough to protect the value of individual courses.
- All tiers are one-time payments with lifetime access — no subscriptions, no upsell pressure inside the course. That simplicity is part of the Zervia promise and keeps refunds and support load low.

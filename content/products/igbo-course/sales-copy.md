## Why Igbo, why now

Igbo is spoken by tens of millions of people — in southeastern Nigeria and in vibrant diaspora communities across the US, UK, and Canada. But for many learners, especially second-generation speakers, the language feels just out of reach: you understand a little, you can follow a joke, but speaking feels impossible.

This course closes that gap. It's built for English speakers starting from zero — or from "I understand but can't speak" — and takes you step by step to holding real, everyday conversations.

## What makes this course different

**Conversational from lesson one.** You won't spend weeks on grammar tables before saying a word. Every lesson teaches phrases you'll actually use — greeting an elder, ordering food, bargaining at the market, introducing yourself at a family event.

**Tones, taught properly.** Igbo is a tonal language — the pitch of your voice changes meaning. Most courses gloss over this; we coach it from day one, with clear explanations and practice drills, so you build good habits instead of guessing.

**Culture woven in, not bolted on.** Language without culture is just vocabulary. You'll learn why greetings matter so much, how titles like *Mazi* and *Da* show respect, what the kola nut ritual means, and the etiquette that keeps you welcome in any Igbo home.

**Real-life scenarios.** Market day. A wedding. A taxi ride. A business meeting. A visit to the family compound. Each module ends with guided conversation practice built around situations you'll genuinely encounter.

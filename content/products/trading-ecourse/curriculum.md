# Trading Foundations — Full Curriculum

*6 modules • self-paced • lifetime access. All lessons are educational content only — no buy/sell recommendations, no price targets.*

> **Disclaimer:** This course is for educational purposes only and is not financial advice. Trading and investing involve risk, including possible loss of principal. Consult a licensed financial advisor before making investment decisions.

---

## Module 1: Trading Foundations ✅ *(written — see module-1-foundations.md)*

The complete beginner baseline: what you're buying, how markets work, where your money sits, and how trades get executed.

1. **What Stocks, ETFs, and Cryptocurrencies Actually Are** — ownership vs. baskets vs. digital tokens, and how each one derives (or doesn't derive) its value.
2. **How Markets Work: Exchanges, Spreads, and Liquidity** — bids, asks, market makers, and why illiquid securities quietly cost you money.
3. **Brokerages and Canadian Account Types** — what a brokerage does, and the tax differences between TFSA, RRSP, and taxable accounts.
4. **Order Types: Market, Limit, and Stop** — how each order behaves, slippage, and why beginners should default to limit orders with a planned stop.
5. **What Moves Prices** — earnings, economic data, news, fund flows, sentiment, and liquidity: the six drivers behind every price change.
6. **Realistic Expectations: Returns, Risk, and the Position-Size Equation** — historical return context, the risk/return trade-off, the 1% risk rule, and why paper trading comes first.

---

## Module 2: Day Trading Basics

What it really means to open and close positions within a single day — the tools, the costs, and the discipline it demands.

1. **What Day Trading Really Is (and Isn't)** — intraday speculation vs. investing; why most day traders lose money and what the survivors do differently.
2. **Reading Candlestick Charts** — open/high/low/close, green vs. red candles, and the handful of patterns worth recognizing (and their limits).
3. **Intraday Price Patterns** — opening range, trend days vs. chop days, and how volume confirms (or undermines) a move.
4. **Level 2 Quotes and Time & Sales** — reading the order book, spotting spoofing and thin walls, and why this data helps but never predicts.
5. **Costs, Rules, and the Pattern Day Trader Consideration** — commissions, spreads, and slippage as a "tax" on every trade; Canadian notes on frequent trading inside registered accounts.
6. **Building a Day-Trading Plan on Paper** — defining your setup, entry, stop, target, and max daily loss — then testing it with a simulator before risking a cent.

---

## Module 3: Swing Trading

Holding positions for days to weeks to capture multi-day moves — slower than day trading, still active, and where most part-time traders land.

1. **Trend vs. Range: Reading Market Structure** — higher highs and higher lows, support and resistance, and why the market's "mode" dictates your approach.
2. **Entry and Exit Frameworks** — pullback entries, breakout entries, and pre-planned exits: entering with a reason and leaving with a rule.
3. **Moving Averages and Momentum Basics** — what moving averages smooth out, golden/death cross context, and why indicators lag price.
4. **Position Sizing for Multi-Day Holds** — wider stops, smaller size: adapting the risk equation from Module 1 to overnight and weekend risk.
5. **Overnight and Gap Risk** — why prices can open far from where they closed, and how swing traders plan for news that lands while markets are shut.
6. **Journaling and Reviewing Swing Trades** — logging setups, screenshots, and emotions; the weekly review habit that turns random trades into a testable process.

---

## Module 4: Long-Term Investing

The buy-and-hold path: building wealth slowly with diversified, low-cost investments — the approach most beginners should start with.

1. **Compounding: The Eighth Wonder, Explained Honestly** — how compounding works, the rule of 72, and why starting early beats timing perfectly.
2. **Index Funds and ETFs for the Long Run** — broad-market exposure, MERs, and why low fees are one of the few reliable predictors of investor outcomes.
3. **Diversification and Asset Allocation** — spreading across asset classes and geographies; matching your stock/bond mix to your time horizon and temperament.
4. **Dollar-Cost Averaging** — investing fixed amounts on a schedule; what it solves (timing anxiety) and what it doesn't (it can't fix a bad asset).
5. **Understanding Fees, Taxes, and Drag** — MERs, trading commissions, foreign withholding tax basics, and how small leaks compound against you.
6. **Building a Plan You Can Actually Stick To** — writing an investment policy statement in plain language: goals, contributions, rebalancing rules, and what you'll do in the next crash.

---

## Module 5: Crypto Essentials

How digital assets work under the hood, why they're so volatile, and how to engage with them without getting wrecked — technically or financially.

1. **Blockchains and Wallets, Plainly Explained** — distributed ledgers, public/private keys, and the difference between holding crypto on an exchange vs. in your own wallet.
2. **Exchanges vs. Self-Custody** — convenience and counterparty risk on exchanges; responsibility and irreversibility of self-custody; no "forgot password" in crypto.
3. **Why Crypto Is So Volatile** — 24/7 trading, thin liquidity, narrative-driven flows, and leverage: the structural reasons behind 20% daily moves.
4. **Stablecoins and Their Role** — dollar-pegged tokens, what "pegged" actually relies on, and why not all stablecoins have held their peg.
5. **Security Basics: Scams, Phishing, and Private Keys** — the most common crypto scams, seed-phrase hygiene, and the rule that no legitimate project ever asks for your keys.
6. **Tax Considerations in Canada** — crypto as property for tax purposes, capital gains vs. business income treatment, and why keeping records from day one saves pain later.

---

## Module 6: Risk Management & Trading Psychology

The module that matters most: the math that keeps you alive and the mindset that keeps you consistent — applicable to every style above.

1. **The 1–2% Risk Rule** — risking a fixed fraction of your account per trade; the math of why small risk survives losing streaks that wipe out big risk.
2. **Stop-Losses and Position Sizing, Applied** — worked examples across stocks, ETFs, and crypto: turning "I'll risk $20" into an exact share count.
3. **Expectancy: How to Know If a Strategy Works** — win rate × average win vs. loss rate × average loss; why a 40%-win strategy can still be profitable.
4. **Fear, Greed, and FOMO** — the emotional cycle of a trade, revenge trading, and pre-commitment devices that protect you from yourself.
5. **The Trading Journal: Your Honest Mirror** — what to record (setup, entry, stop, exit, emotion, lesson) and how to review it without lying to yourself.
6. **Building Rules You Follow on Your Worst Days** — max daily loss limits, cool-down rules, checklists before every trade, and writing the plan when you're calm so it governs you when you're not.

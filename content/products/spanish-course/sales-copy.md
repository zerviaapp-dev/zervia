## What this course actually gives you

**Real conversations, not grammar drills.**
You won't memorize verb conjugation tables. You'll learn how to order food,
ask for directions, check into a hotel, chat with coworkers, and handle a
doctor's visit — the conversations that actually come up in your life.

**Pronunciation you can trust.**
Spanish is wonderfully phonetic: words sound the way they look. We show you the
few sounds that trip up English speakers (that rolled R, the silent H) and drill
them until they feel natural. People will understand you — and tell you so.

**Cultural notes that save you embarrassment.**
When do you use tú and when usted? How many cheek kisses — one, two, or none?
Which words change meaning between Mexico and Spain? We teach the etiquette
alongside the language, so you sound like a respectful guest, not a phrasebook.

**Built for your real scenarios.**
Restaurants, markets, taxis, airports, hotels, pharmacies, offices, family
gatherings. Each module is organized around a situation you'll walk into, with
the exact phrases you need and dialogues showing how they flow together.

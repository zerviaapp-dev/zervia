# Learn Spanish — Pricing

## Suggested tiers

| Tier | Price | What you get |
|------|-------|--------------|
| **Single course** | **$39** | Learn Spanish, full course, lifetime access |
| **3-course bundle** | **$89** | Any 3 language courses (e.g., Spanish + French + Italian), lifetime access |
| **All 9 languages** | **$199** | Every language course in the catalog, lifetime access |

All tiers: one-time payment, lifetime access, 30-day money-back guarantee.

## Rationale

- **$39 base** sits comfortably in the $29–49 impulse-buy band for digital
  courses — low enough to buy on curiosity, high enough to signal real content
  rather than a freebie PDF. It undercuts big-name apps' annual subscriptions
  while offering lifetime ownership, which is the core value proposition.
- **$89 for 3 courses** (~$30/course) rewards the most common real buyer:
  someone learning Spanish who also wants French for a trip or Italian for fun.
  The per-course discount feels generous while lifting average order value well
  above a single sale.
- **$199 for all 9** (~$22/course) targets polyglots, families sharing one
  account, and gift buyers. It frames the catalog as a library rather than a
  one-off purchase, and the headline number still feels attainable.
- **One-time payment, not a subscription** differentiates from Duolingo-style
  apps and matches the marketplace audience: buy it, own it, learn at your pace.
- The 30-day guarantee on every tier removes risk at every price point, which
  matters most at the $199 level.

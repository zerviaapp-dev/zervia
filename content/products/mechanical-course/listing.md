## FAQ

**Do I need any experience?**
No. This course starts from absolute zero. If you've never picked up a wrench, you're exactly who it's for.

**What tools do I need?**
A basic hand tool set is all you need to follow along — a screwdriver set, an adjustable wrench, a hammer, and a tape measure will cover nearly everything in the course. We also teach you what each tool is for and how to choose quality tools without overspending.

**Is this a certification?**
No. This course teaches practical skills and knowledge — it is not a trade certification or license. If you want to become a certified millwright, mechanic, or tradesperson, you'll need formal apprenticeship training. This course gives you the foundation that makes that training easier.

**Will this help me get a job?**
Honestly: it builds a strong foundation, not a credential. If you're applying for entry-level maintenance, helper, or apprenticeship roles, the practical knowledge in this course — tools, drawings, maintenance basics, troubleshooting — will help you sound like you know what you're talking about in an interview and hit the ground running on day one. It won't replace a trade license, but it absolutely shortens the learning curve.

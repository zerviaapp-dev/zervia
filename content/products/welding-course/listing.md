## Description

Welding is one of the most respected skilled trades in the world — but before you strike your first arc, you need to understand what's actually happening: how the processes work, what a good weld looks like, and how defects get spotted before they become failures.

This course is taught from a QA specialist's perspective. Your instructor is a quality professional actively studying welding inspection herself — so you're not getting a dumbed-down overview, you're learning alongside someone who approaches welding with the same professional rigor she brings to her day job on major construction projects. Every module is built around the question an inspector asks: *"What should I be looking for, and why does it matter?"*

Whether your goal is to pick up a welding torch for the first time, move toward a welding inspector role, or just understand the welds you're surrounded by on a job site, this course gives you the foundation most people never get.

## Frequently Asked Questions

**Do I need my own welder to take this course?**
No. This is a theory and knowledge course — you'll learn how each process works, what the equipment does, and how to evaluate welds. When you're ready for hands-on practice, you'll know exactly what to look for in a machine and an instructor.

**Is this a welding certification?**
Honestly: no. This course does not grant a CWB, AWS, or any other welding ticket. Those require supervised, hands-on welding and practical testing. What this course does is build the *knowledge* that certification programs assume you already have — the processes, the terminology, the defect recognition, the inspection basics — so when you do pursue certification, you're ahead, not behind.

**Will this help me become a welding inspector?**
It builds the foundation toward it. Welding inspector certifications (CWI, CWB) require both knowledge and experience. This course gives you the knowledge side: welding processes, WPS reading, defect identification, and visual inspection fundamentals — exactly the concepts inspection exams test. Many people fail inspection courses because they never learned these basics; you won't have that problem.

**Is it safe to learn theory before touching a torch?**
Yes — and it's the smartest order to do it in. Understanding hazards before you're near them is how professionals are trained. That's also why the very first module is safety, and it's required before anything else: PPE, arc flash, fumes, fire, and electrical hazards, all before a single lesson on welding technique.

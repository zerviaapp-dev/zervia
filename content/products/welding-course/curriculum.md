# Welding Fundamentals & Visual Inspection — Course Curriculum

**5 modules · video + text · self-paced**

---

## Module 1: Welding Safety Essentials
*Required first — complete this module before continuing.*

1.1 Why Welding Safety Is Non-Negotiable — the real hazards, real consequences
1.2 PPE Head to Toe — helmets, gloves, jackets, boots, respirators (what to buy, what to avoid)
1.3 Arc Flash & Eye Damage — arc eye, what it is, how to prevent it
1.4 Fume Hazards & Ventilation — manganese, hexavalent chromium, and why airflow matters
1.5 Fire Prevention — hot work, fire watch, extinguishers, and the 30-minute rule
1.6 Electrical Shock Prevention — how welders get shocked and how to make sure you don't
1.7 Safe Work Area Setup & Emergency Procedures

## Module 2: The 4 Processes

2.1 How Arc Welding Actually Works — the fundamentals every process shares
2.2 SMAW (Stick) — how it works, pros/cons, where it's used (field, repair, structural)
2.3 GMAW (MIG) — how it works, pros/cons, where it's used (shops, production, automotive)
2.4 FCAW (Flux-Core) — how it works, pros/cons, where it's used (heavy structural, outdoor work)
2.5 GTAW (TIG) — how it works, pros/cons, where it's used (pipe roots, precision, stainless/aluminum)
2.6 Process Comparison — which process for which job, and why it matters for inspection

## Module 3: Reading a WPS

3.1 What a Welding Procedure Specification Is — and who writes it
3.2 WPS Anatomy — base metal, filler metal, positions, preheat, heat input, and the rest
3.3 Essential Variables — what changes mean a re-qualification, and why inspectors care
3.4 How to Verify a Welder Is Following the WPS — the inspector's practical workflow
3.5 Common WPS Mistakes & Red Flags — what to watch for in the field

## Module 4: Weld Defects & Causes

4.1 Porosity — what it looks like, what causes it, how to prevent it
4.2 Undercut & Overlap — edge defects, their causes, and fixes
4.3 Lack of Fusion — the defect you can't afford to miss
4.4 Cracks — types, causes, and why they're the most serious defect
4.5 Spatter, Slag Inclusion & Other Surface Defects — recognition and prevention
4.6 Defect Prevention Summary — the settings, techniques, and conditions that keep welds clean

## Module 5: Visual Inspection

5.1 The Visual Inspection Checklist — what to check, in what order
5.2 Acceptance Criteria Basics — how codes define "good enough"
5.3 Inspection Tools — weld gauges, lights, mirrors, and how to use them
5.4 Documentation & Reporting — what to record, how to write it up
5.5 Inspection Walkthrough — a full example inspection, start to finish

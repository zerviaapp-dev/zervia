# Learn Tagalog: Speak with Confidence, Connect with Heart

**The beginner-to-conversational course taught in plain English — for real conversations, not textbook drills.**

---

## Why this course exists

Tagalog is spoken by over 80 million people — and if you have Filipino family, friends, coworkers, or travel plans, you've felt the gap: the smile when you say "salamat" right, and the wall when the conversation moves past it.

This course closes that gap. No academic grammar lectures. No memorizing verb conjugation tables you'll never use at a dinner table. Just the words, phrases, and cultural know-how you need to hold real conversations — taught step by step in English.

## What you'll actually be able to do

- **Greet anyone properly** — from "Kumusta?" with friends to the respectful "po" and "opo" with elders
- **Order food like a local** — ask "Anong ulam?", order with confidence, and compliment the cook
- **Shop and bargain** — "Magkano po?" and the friendly haggling phrases markets run on
- **Get around** — directions, jeepneys, tricycles, hotels, and small talk on the road
- **Handle family gatherings** — address ate, kuya, tito, and tita correctly and never feel lost at the table
- **Navigate emergencies** — get help, describe symptoms, and handle the essentials under pressure

## How it's taught

- **Conversational focus** — every module centers on dialogues you'll actually have, broken down line by line
- **Pronunciation that works** — simple romanized phonetics under every phrase; Tagalog vowels are consistent, and we'll prove it to you in Module 1
- **Cultural notes everywhere** — why you say "po", when to do "mano", why refusing food twice matters — because speaking the language without the culture is only half the conversation
- **Real-life scenarios** — the market, the family dinner, the airport, the workplace — practice in the situations you'll actually face
- **Warm, encouraging pace** — lessons are short and practical, designed for busy adults learning a few phrases at a time

## An honest note

No course can make you fluent in 30 days, and we won't pretend this one does. What it will do: give you a solid, confident conversational foundation — the greetings, daily phrases, cultural fluency, and practice habits that turn "I wish I could speak Tagalog" into actual conversations. Fluency comes from using it; this course gets you using it fast.

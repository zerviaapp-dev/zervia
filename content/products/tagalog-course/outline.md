# Learn Tagalog — Full Course Outline
**Beginner to Conversational | Taught in English for English Speakers**

Conversational focus: every lesson ends with a dialogue or drill you can actually use with a Filipino friend, coworker, or family member.

---

## Module 1 — Sound Like You Mean It: Pronunciation & Alphabet
1. The 5 vowels — why Tagalog vowels never lie (a, e, i, o, u always sound the same)
2. The 28-letter alphabet — which letters are borrowed and when you'll actually hear them
3. The glottal stop — the invisible sound that changes "tayo" vs "tayo'"
4. Stress patterns — why MA-buti and ma-BU-ti are different words
5. Reading practice — decode 20 common words out loud with confidence

## Module 2 — Greetings & Introductions
1. Kumusta? — the all-purpose greeting and its 5 natural answers
2. Magandang umaga / hapon / gabi — time-of-day greetings that make you sound local
3. Introducing yourself — "Ako si…" and the 4 questions that always follow
4. Po and opo — the politeness particles that open every Filipino door
5. Goodbyes that aren't goodbyes — paalam, sige, ingat, and when to use each
6. First-meeting mini conversations — 3 real dialogues, line by line

## Module 3 — Numbers, Time & Dates
1. Numbers 1–100 — cardinals, and how to say prices, ages, and quantities
2. Ordinals and counting people/things — una, pangalawa, ikalawa
3. Telling time — "alas tres" and the Filipino way of rounding the clock
4. Days, months, seasons — plus how birthdays and holidays are celebrated
5. Useful phrases — "Ilang minuto?", "Anong oras?", "Mabilis ba?"

## Module 4 — Food & Dining
1. At the table — kanin, ulam, sabaw: the anatomy of a Filipino meal
2. Ordering food — "Pabili po ng…" and how to ask what's available
3. The magic question — "Anong ulam?" and 25 must-know dish names
4. Drinks and merienda — kape, buko juice, halo-halo, and snack culture
5. Complimenting the cook — "Ang sarap!" and other phrases that win hearts
6. Dietary needs — vegetarian, allergies, "walang karne" and how to say no politely

## Module 5 — Shopping & Money
1. Money words — piso, pera, sukli, and how prices are spoken
2. How much? — "Magkano po?" plus the 6 haggling phrases markets expect
3. Bargaining with a smile — the friendly art of "tawad"
4. Sizes, colors, quantities — "May mas malaki po ba?"
5. At the counter — paying, change, receipts, "paki…" requests
6. Online shopping phrases — chat-speak you'll see on Filipino marketplaces

## Module 6 — Travel & Directions
1. Asking where — "Nasaan po ang…?" and landmark-based navigation
2. Direction words — kaliwa, kanan, diretso, and the famous "doon lang"
3. Transportation talk — jeep, tricycle, "para po!" and fares
4. At the airport and hotel — check-in, "May reservation po ako"
5. Small talk on the road — what drivers and vendors love to chat about

## Module 7 — Family & Social Life
1. Family vocabulary — nanay, tatay, lolo, lola, tito, tita (and why everyone's an "auntie")
2. Addressing people — ate, kuya, and the respect system behind nicknames
3. Talking about yourself — work, hometown, "Taga-saan ka?"
4. Making friends — invitations, "Tara!", weekend plans
5. Compliments and small talk — "Ang ganda ng…", weather chat that never fails
6. Saying thanks and sorry — salamat, pasensya na, and levels of apology

## Module 8 — Work & Business Phrases
1. Workplace introductions — titles, "boss", and respectful address at work
2. Meetings and requests — "Paki-send po", polite emails and messages
3. Phone calls — "Hello po, sino po sila?", taking a message
4. Deadlines and follow-ups — "Kailan po?", "Naipadala na po ba?"
5. Small talk with colleagues — safe topics, break-time chat

## Module 9 — Emergencies & Health
1. Getting help — "Tulong!", "Kailangan ko ng tulong"
2. At the clinic — symptoms, "Masakit ang…", body parts
3. Pharmacy phrases — "May gamot po ba para sa…?"
4. Important numbers and asking for the police — "Nasaan ang presinto?"
5. Travel insurance and documents — "Nawala ang passport ko"

## Module 10 — Culture, Etiquette & Conversation Practice
1. Mano po and other gestures — respect in action
2. Hospitality rules — why you never refuse food twice
3. Taboos and sensitivities — topics to handle gently
4. Fiestas, holidays, and "Filipino time" — what to actually expect
5. Conversation lab 1 — at the market (full dialogue, word by word)
6. Conversation lab 2 — meeting your partner's Filipino family
7. Conversation lab 3 — a full day out in Manila, start to finish
8. Your 90-day practice plan — how to keep speaking after the course ends

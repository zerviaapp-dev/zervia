# Learn Tagalog — Pricing

## Tiers

| Tier | Price | Includes |
|---|---|---|
| **Single Course** | **$39** | Full Learn Tagalog course (10 modules), lifetime access |
| **3-Course Bundle** | **$89** | Tagalog + any 2 other language courses |
| **All 9 Languages** | **$199** | Every language course in the Zervia library, lifetime access |

## Rationale

- **$39 single course** sits in the sweet spot of the $29–49 range: affordable enough for an impulse buy, high enough to signal a serious, complete course rather than a throwaway app lesson pack.
- **$89 three-course bundle** rewards the most common buying pattern — learners rarely stop at one language (Tagalog + Spanish, Tagalog + French, etc.). Effective price of ~$30/course keeps the single-course price honest while lifting average order value.
- **$199 all-9-languages** is the anchor offer for serious polyglots and families (e.g., couples learning together). At ~$22/language it's a clear steal versus buying separately, and it positions Zervia as a full language library, not a one-course shop.
- No subscriptions, no upsell traps: one-time payment, lifetime access. Matches the "honest, no hype" brand promise in the sales copy.

# QA/QC Quality Course for Construction & Industrial Projects — Curriculum

## Module 1: Quality Foundations
1. What "Quality" Means on a Construction Project — Why It Matters and What Rework Really Costs
2. QC vs QA vs QM — Three Levels of Quality, Explained with Examples
3. The Quality Triangle — Owner, Contractor, and Inspector Roles; Prevention vs Detection Economics
4. Quality Culture and Why Paperwork Matters — A Real NCR Story (Anonymized)
5. Key Terminology Glossary + A Day in the Life of a QA/QC Inspector

## Module 2: Reading Inspection Test Plans — ITPs
1. What an ITP Is — The Backbone of Every Quality Program
2. Hold Points, Witness Points, and Review Points — Who Stops, Who Watches, Who Checks
3. How to Follow an ITP on Site — Step by Step Through a Real Inspection Sequence
4. Creating a Simple ITP — Building One from Scratch Using the Included Template

## Module 3: The NCR Process
1. What Triggers an NCR — Non-Conformance vs. Defect vs. Deviation
2. Writing a Clear Non-Conformance Report — Structure, Facts, and a Real Example
3. Disposition — Repair, Rework, Reject, or Use-As-Is (And Who Decides)
4. Closing the Loop — Verification, Sign-Off, and Trending into Prevention

## Module 4: Weld Inspection Basics
1. The 4 Welding Processes at a Glance — SMAW, GMAW, FCAW, GTAW
2. Reading a WPS — What a Welding Procedure Specification Tells You
3. Common Weld Defects — Porosity, Undercut, Lack of Fusion, Cracks: Appearance and Causes
4. The Visual Inspection Checklist — The Field Walk-Through, Step by Step (Included Template)
5. When to Call the Welding Inspector — Your Limits and the Chain of Escalation

## Module 5: Documentation & Professional Practice
1. Daily Inspection Reports — What to Record and How to Write Them
2. Traceability — Material Certs, Heat Numbers, and Following the Paper Trail
3. Turnover Dossiers — Building the Package That Hands a Project to the Owner
4. Dealing with Contractors Diplomatically — Firm on Standards, Professional in Tone
5. Career Paths in QA/QC — Where This Skill Takes You Next

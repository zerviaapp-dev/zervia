## FAQ

**Do I need experience in construction to take this course?**
No. Module 1 starts from zero — it explains what quality means on a construction project, defines every key term (ITP, NCR, hold point, surveillance, audit), and shows you what an inspector actually does day-to-day. If you have construction experience, you'll move faster and the examples will click immediately. If you don't, the course is built so you can still follow it from the beginning. What helps most is a genuine interest in how things are built and how quality is enforced.

**Is this a certification?**
No — and we won't pretend it is. There is no accredited credential attached to this course, and claiming otherwise would be dishonest. What you get is something different and, for getting hired, often more useful: genuine practical knowledge of how quality works on real industrial projects. You'll understand ITPs, NCRs, weld inspection basics, and documentation the way working inspectors understand them — the working knowledge employers actually test in interviews and value on site. If you later pursue formal inspector certifications (such as CWB or AWS credentials for weld inspection), this course gives you a serious head start on the fundamentals.

**Will this help me get a QA job?**
Honestly: it builds the real working knowledge that employers value, which is the single most important thing you can bring to a QA/QC interview or your first inspection role. Companies hiring QA/QC inspectors look for people who understand ITPs, can write a clear NCR, know what weld defects look like, and document their work properly — exactly what this course teaches. What no course can do is hand you a job: you'll still need to apply, interview, and prove yourself in the field. But you'll walk in speaking the language and thinking like an inspector, which puts you far ahead of candidates who only know quality from a textbook.

**How is this different from free YouTube content?**
YouTube has good quality content — but it's fragmented. A 10-minute video on NCRs here, a weld defect video there, nothing connecting them into the complete system an inspector actually works inside. This course is a structured curriculum designed by someone who does this work daily on major industrial projects: foundations first, then ITPs, then the NCR process, then weld inspection, then documentation and professional practice — in the order a working inspector learns them. You also get downloadable templates (ITP, NCR, weld inspection checklist) you can use in the real world. Free content teaches you facts. This teaches you the job.

**Can I ask questions as I go?**
Yes. The course includes community Q&A — post your questions and get answers from the instructor team, and learn from other students' questions too. Real ITP and NCR questions from your own projects are especially welcome.

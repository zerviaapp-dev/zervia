# Construction Safety Essentials — Course Curriculum

**4 modules · video + text · self-paced**

---

## Module 1: Safety Mindset & PPE

1.1 The Real Cost of Incidents — human and financial consequences, honestly framed
1.2 Safety Culture vs. Compliance — why rules alone don't save lives
1.3 PPE Deep Dive — hard hats, eye & face, hearing, respiratory, gloves, hi-vis, boots: selection, fit, inspection, replacement
1.4 PPE Inspection Routines — the daily checks that catch failures before they matter
1.5 Common PPE Mistakes Seen on Sites — and how to avoid every one of them
1.6 Your Rights — the right to know, the right to participate, the right to refuse unsafe work (Canadian framework)

## Module 2: Fall Protection Basics

2.1 Why Falls Kill — understanding fall hazards on construction sites
2.2 Guardrails & Passive Protection — what good guardrails look like
2.3 Harnesses — inspection basics and how to don a harness correctly
2.4 Lanyards, Anchor Points & Fall Clearance — the system that catches you, and what makes it fail
2.5 Ladder Safety — selection, setup, climbing, and the three points of contact
2.6 Scaffold Awareness — what to check before stepping onto a scaffold

## Module 3: Hazard Identification

3.1 Struck-By Hazards — the most common killer, and how to stay clear
3.2 Caught-Between Hazards — pinch points, trenches, equipment
3.3 Electrical Hazards on Site — overhead lines, temporary power, and the distance rules
3.4 Trenching & Excavation Awareness — why trenches collapse and what a safe one looks like
3.5 Job Hazard Assessments — how to break a task down and spot the hazards in each step
3.6 Stop-Work Authority — everyone's right and duty to stop unsafe work

## Module 4: Communication & Reporting

4.1 Toolbox Talks — what they are, why they work, and how to run one (template included)
4.2 Near-Miss Reporting — why near misses are free lessons, and how to report them
4.3 Incident Reporting Steps — what to do, in what order, when something goes wrong (template included)
4.4 Emergency Response Basics — alarms, muster points, first aid, and who calls whom
4.5 Building a Safety Habit — carrying these practices onto every site you'll ever work

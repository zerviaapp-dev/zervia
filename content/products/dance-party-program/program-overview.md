# Dance Party — Program Overview

**4 weeks · 12 sessions · 45 minutes each · 3 sessions per week**

Every session follows the same party structure: 5-minute warm-up track → 35 minutes of song-by-song dance routines (8–10 songs) with water breaks → 5-minute cool-down and stretch. Intensity builds across the 4 weeks, but low-impact options stay available in every song, every session.

**Intensity key:** 🟢 Low · 🟡 Moderate · 🔴 High

---

## Week 1 — Latin Basics: Merengue & Salsa

**Playlist theme:** The two friendliest rhythms in Latin dance.
**Rhythm focus:** Merengue (marching 2-step, side steps) and Salsa (basic step, side-to-side). Simple, repetitive, impossible to get lost.
**Session structure:** Longer warm-up with step demos; each new step is taught slowly before the music kicks in; generous water breaks between songs.

- **Session 1 — "Meet the Beat":** Learn the merengue march and salsa basic. 8 songs alternating merengue/salsa. Instructor pauses to demo each new step. Ends with a slow cool-down song and full-body stretch.
- **Session 2 — "Side to Side":** Add merengue side steps, salsa side basics, and simple arm styling. 9 songs. Slightly faster transitions between songs.
- **Session 3 — "First Full Party":** Put it all together — a continuous 35-minute dance block with minimal pauses, mixing everything learned. 9 songs. Celebrate with the first "freestyle" 30 seconds (dance however you like!).

**Week 1 goal:** March a merengue and step a salsa basic without thinking. Survive — and enjoy — a full 45 minutes.

---

## Week 2 — Around the World: Cumbia & Reggaeton

**Playlist theme:** Travel the Latin world without leaving your living room.
**Rhythm focus:** Cumbia (the loping Colombian step, sleepy-leg groove) and Reggaeton (dembow beat, hip-driven moves, bigger energy).
**Session structure:** Shorter demos (you're getting the hang of it); cumbia songs lean 🟢–🟡, reggaeton pushes 🟡–🔴. Core and hip work woven in.

- **Session 1 — "Cumbia Groove":** Learn the cumbia basic (forward-back step with a sway) and the "sleepy leg" variation. 8 songs, mostly cumbia with a merengue throwback. Focus: hips and rhythm.
- **Session 2 — "Reggaeton Fire":** Learn the dembow stomp, reggaeton hip rolls, and the "perreo-lite" bounce (PG and fun). 9 songs, high energy. Focus: cardio and confidence.
- **Session 3 — "World Tour":** Cumbia + reggaeton + Week 1 favourites in one continuous party. 10 songs. First appearance of a "shout-out round" — instructor calls out steps, you execute.

**Week 2 goal:** Switch rhythms mid-song without panic. Feel your cardio fitness climbing.

---

## Week 3 — Cardio Party: Samba, Soca & Hip-Hop Fusion

**Playlist theme:** Carnival energy. The sweatiest, happiest week.
**Rhythm focus:** Samba (bouncy Brazilian step, fast feet), Soca (Caribbean jump-and-wave energy), and a Hip-Hop fusion track (body rolls, grooves, attitude).
**Session structure:** Minimal demos — learn by following. Two 🔴 peak songs per session with a recovery song after each. Extra water break before the final block.

- **Session 1 — "Samba Bounce":** Learn the samba basic (bounce-step with quick feet) and volta steps. 9 songs. Focus: legs and stamina.
- **Session 2 — "Soca Jump-Up":** Learn the soca march, "wuk-up" hip circles, and flag-wave arms. 9 songs, biggest cardio of the program so far. Focus: full-body burn.
- **Session 3 — "Fusion Night":** Hip-hop fusion meets Latin — body rolls, step-touches with attitude, and a dance-battle-style freestyle finale. 10 songs. Focus: letting loose.

**Week 3 goal:** Dance two 🔴 songs back-to-back and live to tell the tale. Notice your stamina vs. Week 1.

---

## Week 4 — Dance Celebration: Mix & Cool-Down Flow

**Playlist theme:** Greatest hits + a victory lap.
**Rhythm focus:** A mix of all six rhythms, plus a flowing cool-down routine that feels like a moving meditation.
**Session structure:** Sessions 1–2 are "best of" parties voted by the program's structure (the most-loved songs return). Session 3 is the graduation celebration: full party + extended cool-down flow + reflection.

- **Session 1 — "Greatest Hits I":** The best merengue, salsa, and cumbia routines return — now danced with confidence. 10 songs.
- **Session 2 — "Greatest Hits II":** The best reggaeton, samba, and soca routines return. 10 songs. Peak cardio of the whole program.
- **Session 3 — "Graduation Party":** A 30-minute all-rhythms celebration medley, then a 10-minute cool-down flow (slow Latin grooves + deep stretching + breathing). End with a moment of reflection on 4 weeks of showing up.

**Week 4 goal:** Dance the full party like you own it. Feel the difference 12 sessions made — in your body, your energy, and your mood.

---

## After the program

Replay any week, build your own party from favourite sessions, or run the whole 4 weeks again — you'll be amazed how much easier Week 1 feels the second time. Lifetime access means the party never has to end. 🎉

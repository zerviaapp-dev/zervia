# Dance Party — Week 1: Latin Basics (FULL SESSION PLANS)

**Session rhythm:** 5-min warm-up → ~35-min dance block (8–10 songs, water breaks included) → 5-min cool-down + stretch.

**Intensity key:** 🟢 Low · 🟡 Moderate · 🔴 High

**Instructor notes:** Demo every new step slowly before the song starts. Call out the next move 4 counts early ("side step coming — 5, 6, 7, 8!"). Demo the low-impact version side-by-side in the first song of each new rhythm. Energy is everything — smile, whoop, and mean it.

**Low-impact legend:** LI = low-impact version of the move.

---

# Session 1 — "Meet the Beat"

*Goal: Learn the merengue march and the salsa basic. Leave sweaty and smiling.*

## Warm-up (5 min)

**Track 1 — "Wake Up the Body" | Merengue | ~120 BPM | 🟢**
- *Choreography:* March in place (8 counts) → step-touch right and left (8 counts) → shoulder rolls while marching (8 counts) → gentle side reaches (8 counts). Repeat the loop.
- *Coaching cues:* "Good morning, beautiful people! March it out — nice and easy. Find the beat in your feet. Shoulders back, chin up, smile ON!"
- *LI:* Already low-impact. Smaller steps if needed.

## Dance Block (~35 min)

**Track 2 — "Merengue March" | Merengue | ~125 BPM | 🟢**
- *New step taught:* **Merengue basic** — march in place, shifting weight fully from foot to foot. Hips sway naturally.
- *Choreography (8-count blocks):* March in place (16) → march with hip sway, hands on hips (16) → march forward 4, back 4 (16) → repeat.
- *Cues:* "Left, right, left, right — that's it! Let those hips talk a little. You're doing it!"
- *LI:* March in place with smaller steps, no forward/back travel.

**💧 Water break — 30 seconds.** "Hydrate, gorgeous! Shake it out."

**Track 3 — "Salsa Time" | Salsa | ~130 BPM | 🟢**
- *New step taught:* **Salsa basic** — step forward on 1, rock back on 2, step together on 3, hold 4; then back on 5, forward on 6, together on 7, hold 8. (Simplified: forward-back-together, back-forward-together.)
- *Choreography:* Salsa basic slow (32 counts) → salsa basic with a clap on the hold (32) → repeat.
- *Cues:* "Forward, back, together — hold! Feel that? That's salsa! Don't worry about perfect — worry about moving."
- *LI:* Step-touch in place to the beat instead of the rock step.

**Track 4 — "Side by Side" | Merengue | ~128 BPM | 🟡**
- *New step taught:* **Merengue side step** — step right, together, step right, touch (8 counts); mirror left.
- *Choreography:* Side step right (8) → side step left (8) → merengue march in place (16) → repeat with arm styling (arms sweep up on the side step).
- *Cues:* "Step, together, step, TOUCH! Arms up like you just don't care! Woo!"
- *LI:* Smaller side steps, arms at shoulder height.

**💧 Water break — 30 seconds.**

**Track 5 — "Salsa Side Basic" | Salsa | ~132 BPM | 🟡**
- *New step taught:* **Salsa side basic** — step right, rock left behind, replace; mirror. (Side-together-side, hold.)
- *Choreography:* Side basic right (16) → side basic left (16) → salsa basic forward/back (16) → freestyle arms for 8 → repeat.
- *Cues:* "Side, rock, replace — yes! You're dancing salsa, my friend!"
- *LI:* Step-touch side to side, no rock step.

**Track 6 — "Merengue Party Mix" | Merengue | ~130 BPM | 🟡**
- *Choreography:* Merengue march (16) → side steps (16) → march forward 4/back 4 (16) → all with big arm styling: hands up, snap on the 8.
- *Cues:* "This is YOUR party! Bigger arms! Show me that smile!"
- *LI:* Keep feet marching in place; arms do the partying.

**💧 Water break — 45 seconds.** "Almost there — two songs of pure fun left!"

**Track 7 — "Beginner's Salsa Groove" | Salsa | ~135 BPM | 🟡**
- *Choreography:* Salsa basic (32) → side basic (32) → alternating every 16 counts. Instructor calls the changes: "Forward basic — 5, 6, 7, 8! Side basic — 5, 6, 7, 8!"
- *Cues:* "Listen for my call! You've got this — two moves, that's all!"
- *LI:* Step-touch throughout; follow the direction calls only.

**Track 8 — "First Fiesta" | Merengue + Salsa medley | ~130 BPM | 🟡**
- *Choreography:* 32 counts merengue march/side steps → 32 counts salsa basics → repeat. Final 16 counts: FREESTYLE — dance however you want!
- *Cues:* "Last song! Everything you learned! And now — FREESTYLE! Nobody's watching — GO!"
- *LI:* Freestyle at your own level. Marching with style counts.

## Cool-down + Stretch (5 min)

**Track 9 — "Slow Down" | Slow Latin ballad | ~90 BPM | 🟢**
- *Choreography:* Slow side steps → gentle arm circles → slow march shrinking to stillness → standing stretches: neck tilts, shoulder stretch across the body, standing quad stretch (hold a wall), calf stretch against the wall, deep side stretch, forward fold with bent knees.
- *Cues:* "Slow it down... deep breath in... and let it all go. You just did your first dance-fitness class. How amazing are you? Roll those shoulders — thank your body. See you next session!"
- Hold each stretch 20–30 seconds, breathing deeply.

# Session 2 — "Side to Side"

*Goal: Add merengue side steps with styling, salsa side basics with turns prep. Slightly faster transitions.*

## Warm-up (5 min)

**Track 1 — "Warm-Up Groove" | Merengue | ~122 BPM | 🟢**
- *Choreography:* March in place (16) → step-touch with shoulder shimmies (16) → side reaches with a bounce (16) → repeat.
- *Cues:* "Welcome back, party people! You survived Day 1 — today we level up just a tiny bit. March it out!"

## Dance Block (~35 min)

**Track 2 — "Merengue Review" | Merengue | ~126 BPM | 🟢**
- *Choreography:* Merengue march (16) → side steps (16) → forward 4/back 4 (16) → repeat. Quick review, no new steps.
- *Cues:* "Show me what you remember! March — side step — forward and back! Yes!"

**💧 Water break — 30 seconds.**

**Track 3 — "Styling It Up" | Merengue | ~130 BPM | 🟡**
- *New:* **Arm styling** — on side steps, sweep arms overhead in a V; on the march, add shoulder shimmies.
- *Choreography:* Side step right with arms in V (8) → left (8) → march with shoulder shimmy (16) → repeat.
- *Cues:* "Arms UP — big V! Like you won something! Shoulders — shimmy, shimmy!"
- *LI:* Arms at shoulder height; march without shimmy.

**Track 4 — "Salsa Suave" | Salsa | ~132 BPM | 🟡**
- *Choreography:* Salsa basic (32) → side basic (32) → repeat with arm styling (hands float at waist height, fingers snapping softly).
- *Cues:* "Smooth... smooth... Feel elegant. Salsa is cool, calm, and confident."

**💧 Water break — 30 seconds.**

**Track 5 — "Turn It (Prep)" | Salsa | ~134 BPM | 🟡**
- *New:* **Quarter turns** — during the salsa basic, turn 90° to face a new wall every 8 counts. (Full spot turns are Week 3 — today is just quarters.)
- *Choreography:* Salsa basic facing front (16) → quarter turn right, continue basic (16) → quarter turn, continue (16) → quarter turn back to front (16).
- *Cues:* "Turn — 2, 3, 4! New wall, same step! Don't overthink — your feet know what to do."
- *LI:* Skip the turns; keep dancing facing front.

**Track 6 — "Merengue con Sabor" | Merengue | ~132 BPM | 🟡**
- *Choreography:* Side steps with hip emphasis (16) → march with double-time feet for 8 counts (a little burst!) → back to regular march (16) → repeat.
- *Cues:* "Hips! HIPS! Now quick feet — go go go — and recover! That's your first cardio burst — you loved it, admit it!"
- *LI:* Skip the double-time burst; keep a steady march.

**💧 Water break — 45 seconds.** "Two songs left — finish strong!"

**Track 7 — "Salsa Loca (a little)" | Salsa | ~136 BPM | 🟡**
- *Choreography:* Alternate forward basic and side basic every 16 counts, with quarter turns sprinkled in. Instructor calls everything.
- *Cues:* "Forward! Side! Turn! You're doing THREE things now — look at you!"
- *LI:* Pick one move and stay with it. Following along matters more than keeping up.

**Track 8 — "Party Closer" | Merengue | ~130 BPM | 🟡**
- *Choreography:* Free combination — instructor mixes all Week 1 moves. Last 30 seconds: FREESTYLE.
- *Cues:* "Everything! March, side step, salsa, styling — MIX IT! Freestyle — show me something!"
- *LI:* Dance your version. There is no wrong.

## Cool-down + Stretch (5 min)

**Track 9 — "Breathe Easy" | Slow acoustic Latin | ~88 BPM | 🟢**
- Same stretch sequence as Session 1, plus a standing figure-4 hip stretch (hold a wall) and wrist circles.
- *Cues:* "Deep breath in... let the shoulders drop... You are TWO sessions in. Your body is learning a new language. Be proud."

# Session 3 — "First Full Party"

*Goal: Dance a continuous 35-minute block with minimal pauses. Celebrate Week 1!*

## Warm-up (5 min)

**Track 1 — "Party Warm-Up" | Merengue | ~124 BPM | 🟢**
- *Choreography:* March (16) → step-touch with big arm circles (16) → side steps (16) → gentle bounce in place (16).
- *Cues:* "Day 3! This is your party now — I just work here! Warm it up!"

## Dance Block (~35 min) — minimal pauses, instructor demos on the fly

**Track 2 — "Apertura" | Merengue | ~128 BPM | 🟡**
- *Choreography:* March (16) → side steps with V arms (16) → forward 4/back 4 with claps (16) → repeat.
- *Cues:* "Opening number! Big energy from the top!"

**Track 3 — "Salsita" | Salsa | ~132 BPM | 🟡**
- *Choreography:* Forward basic (32) → side basic (32) → quarter-turn basics (32).
- *Cues:* "Salsa block! Basics — side — turn! Flow with it!"

**💧 Water break — 30 seconds.** "Quick sip — we're rolling!"

**Track 4 — "Merengue Madness" | Merengue | ~132 BPM | 🔴**
- *Choreography:* Side steps double-time for 16 counts (cardio push!) → recover with march (16) → repeat ×3.
- *Cues:* "PUSH! Quick feet — 16 counts — GO! And recover... Feel that heart? That's fitness happening!"
- *LI:* Keep the march steady through the push sections.

**Track 5 — "Salsa Caliente" | Salsa | ~136 BPM | 🟡**
- *Choreography:* Continuous basics alternating forward/side every 16, styling arms throughout, one quarter-turn sequence.
- *Cues:* "Hot salsa! Smooth and spicy!"

**💧 Water break — 30 seconds.**

**Track 6 — "Fiesta Mix I" | Merengue | ~130 BPM | 🟡**
- *Choreography:* Instructor's choice medley of all merengue moves. Call-outs only, no pauses.
- *Cues:* "No stopping now! Follow my voice — march! Side! V-arms! Shimmy!"

**Track 7 — "Fiesta Mix II" | Salsa | ~134 BPM | 🟡**
- *Choreography:* Same concept, all salsa moves. 30-second freestyle in the middle.
- *Cues:* "Salsa medley! And — FREESTYLE! 30 seconds — nobody's watching!"

**💧 Water break — 30 seconds.** "Last song of Week 1 — make it count!"

**Track 8 — "Gran Final" | Merengue + Salsa medley | ~132 BPM | 🔴**
- *Choreography:* 16 counts of your FAVOURITE move → 16 of your second favourite → repeat, building to a final freestyle finish with hands in the air.
- *Cues:* "YOUR favourite moves! Pick 'em! This is YOUR dance! HANDS UP! WEEK ONE — DONE!!!"
- *LI:* Your favourite low-impact moves. Hands up counts as cardio.

## Cool-down + Stretch (5 min)

**Track 9 — "Celebración Lenta" | Slow romantic salsa | ~85 BPM | 🟢**
- Extended stretch: all previous stretches + a full 60 seconds of slow side-to-side sway with deep breathing to close.
- *Cues:* "Slow... sway... breathe... You just finished WEEK ONE of Dance Party. Three full dance sessions. Give yourself the biggest round of applause — you earned it. Week 2 brings cumbia and reggaeton — new rhythms, same party. I'll see you there!"

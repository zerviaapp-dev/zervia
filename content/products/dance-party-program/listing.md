## FAQ

**I have two left feet. Can I really do dance-fitness?**
Yes — that's exactly who this program is for. Week 1 teaches just two rhythms with simple, repetitive steps. The moves repeat every 8 counts, so even if you're lost for the first 30 seconds of a song, you'll catch on. Nobody's watching, nobody's judging — and "messing up" just means you're dancing freestyle.

**Are there low-impact options? My knees can't take jumping.**
Every routine includes a low-impact track: march in place instead of jumping, step-touch instead of hopping, keep one foot on the floor during turns. The instructor demos the low-impact version alongside the full version in every session. Your knees will thank you.

**How fast will I see results?**
Most people feel a difference in energy and mood after the first week. Visible toning and fitness gains typically show up around weeks 3–4 if you do all 3 sessions per week — and the real results come from sticking with it. This program is designed to make you *want* to keep dancing, which is the whole secret.

**Do I need music equipment or a big sound system?**
Nope. Your phone, tablet, or laptop speaker works fine to start. If you want to feel the bass more, any Bluetooth speaker does the trick. The program's music is built into each video session — you just press play.

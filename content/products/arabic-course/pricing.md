# Arabic Course — Pricing

## Tiers

| Package | Price | What's included |
|---|---|---|
| **Single course — Learn Arabic** | **$49** | Full 10-module Arabic ecourse, lifetime access |
| **3-course bundle** | **$109** | Arabic + any 2 other Zervia language courses |
| **All 9 languages** | **$249** | The complete Zervia language library, lifetime access |

## Rationale

- **$49 single-course price** sits at the top of the standard $29–49 language-course range. Arabic commands a premium: it's widely perceived as one of the hardest languages for English speakers (new script, unfamiliar sounds), so learners expect — and willingly pay for — a course that takes the script seriously. Pricing below the pack would signal "phrasebook," not a real course.
- **$109 for 3 courses** (~$36/course) rewards learners who pair Arabic with a companion language (e.g., French for North Africa, Spanish, or another in the library) while keeping per-course value above the $29 floor.
- **$249 for all 9 languages** (~$28/course) is the anchor offer for serious polyglots and heritage families; it makes the bundle feel like a library investment rather than nine separate purchases, and it lifts average order value.
- All tiers are **one-time payments with lifetime access** — no subscriptions, no upsell pressure. Simple pricing matches the honest, no-hype positioning of the course itself.

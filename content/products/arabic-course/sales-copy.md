# Learn Arabic: Beginner to Conversational

**Speak real Arabic — in the script, with real people, from your very first lesson.**

You've thought about learning Arabic before, and one thing stopped you: the script. Those beautiful, flowing letters look impossible to a Latin-alphabet brain.

Here's the truth: the Arabic alphabet has 28 letters — fewer than English's 26 plus our messy exceptions — and it's *phonetic*. Once you learn the letters, you can read almost anything out loud. This course starts there, with the script, and builds you up lesson by lesson until you're ordering food, haggling in the souq, introducing yourself, and handling travel days in Arabic.

## What makes this course different

- **Script-first, fear-free.** We don't hide the Arabic script behind transliteration forever. You learn to read it from Module 1 — gently, with transliteration beside it as training wheels that come off naturally.
- **Modern Standard Arabic, understood everywhere.** MSA is what news, books, and formal settings use across 22 countries. Where everyday speech differs, we flag it: Egyptian, Levantine, and Gulf dialect notes are woven in, so you're never confused when a taxi driver in Cairo sounds different from your textbook.
- **Real-life scenarios, not grammar drills.** Every module ends in situations you'll actually use: the restaurant, the souq, the taxi, the hotel, the family dinner. You'll learn the special sounds — ع ح ق — with mouth-position coaching, not linguistics lectures.
- **Cultural notes that matter.** Arabic and Arab culture are inseparable: when to say *as-salāmu ʿalaykum*, how to respond to it, why you always eat with your right hand, what *mā shāʾ Allāh* signals. You'll speak well *and* act well.

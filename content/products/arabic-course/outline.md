# Learn Arabic — Beginner to Conversational
*Taught entirely in English for English speakers. Modern Standard Arabic (MSA) with Egyptian, Levantine, and Gulf dialect notes.*

---

## Module 1 — The Arabic Alphabet & Sounds
Learn the 28-letter script from scratch — reading and writing right-to-left.

1. **Meet the Alphabet** — The 28 letters in isolation; letter names and where Arabic is spoken.
2. **Reading Right-to-Left** — Letter shapes that change at the start, middle, and end of words.
3. **The Vowel Marks** — Fatḥa, kasra, ḍamma, and sukūn; why short vowels are usually invisible.
4. **Sounds English Doesn't Have** — ع (ʿayn), ح (ḥāʾ), ق (qāf), غ (ghayn) with mouth-position coaching.
5. **Emphatic Letters** — ص ض ط ظ: the "heavy" sounds that make the same words mean different things.
6. **Letters That Connect Differently** — ا د ذ ر ز و: letters that never connect to what follows.
7. **Writing Practice** — Forming your first words: salaam, shukran, kitab.
8. **Module Quiz** — Read 10 transliteration-free words aloud.

## Module 2 — Greetings & Introductions
The phrases you'll use every single day.

1. **As-salāmu ʿalaykum & Friends** — The universal greeting and its responses.
2. **Hello, Welcome, Goodbye** — Marḥaban, ahlan wa sahlan, maʿa as-salāma.
3. **Introducing Yourself** — "My name is…", "I am from…", "I am American/Canadian".
4. **Small Talk Basics** — "How are you?" (formal and informal), "What's new?"
5. **Please, Thank You, You're Welcome** — Shukran, min faḍlik, ʿafwan — with when to use each.
6. **Morning, Evening, Night** — Time-of-day greetings: ṣabāḥ al-khayr, masāʾ al-khayr.
7. **Yes, No, Maybe** — Naʿam, lā, mumken — with polite softeners.
8. **First Conversation Drill** — Put it all together in a 30-second exchange.

## Module 3 — Numbers, Time & Dates
Count, tell time, and handle dates like a local.

1. **Numbers 1–20** — Counting the Arabic way, with script and pronunciation.
2. **Tens, Hundreds & Thousands** — Building big numbers for prices and addresses.
3. **Telling Time** — "It's three o'clock", quarter and half past, AM/PM.
4. **Days of the Week** — From al-aḥad (Sunday) through as-sabt (Saturday).
5. **Months & Dates** — Islamic and Gregorian months; "Today is…".
6. **Useful Time Words** — Yesterday, today, tomorrow, morning, evening, now, later.
7. **Asking "How Much?"** — Numbers in action: prices, taxi fares, bargaining openers.

## Module 4 — Food & Dining
Eat well anywhere in the Arab world.

1. **At the Restaurant** — "A table for two, please"; reading a menu in Arabic.
2. **Ordering Food & Drink** — "I would like…", common dishes, drinks, no spice please.
3. **Dietary Needs** — Vegetarian, allergies, halal questions.
4. **Compliments to the Cook** — "The food is delicious"; thanking your host.
5. **Café Culture** — Ordering Arabic coffee (qahwa) and mint tea like a regular.
6. **Dialect Food Words** — Egyptian vs Levantine vs Gulf names for bread, eggs, water.
7. **Paying the Bill** — "The check, please"; tipping customs.

## Module 5 — Shopping & Money (The Souq Experience)
Haggle with confidence and a smile.

1. **Essential Shopping Phrases** — "How much is this?", "I'm just looking", "Too expensive".
2. **Bargaining in the Souq** — The friendly back-and-forth, step by step.
3. **Numbers at Speed** — Hearing and understanding prices quickly.
4. **Sizes, Colors, Materials** — Describing what you want: bigger, smaller, this color.
5. **Money & Currency** — Rial, dirham, dinar, pound; "Do you take cards?"
6. **Market Small Talk** — Compliments and humor that get you the local price.
7. **Polite Walk-Aways** — Saying no gracefully; the "I'll come back" move.

## Module 6 — Travel & Directions
Get around cities, airports, and taxis.

1. **At the Airport** — Arrivals, baggage, "Where is…?" essentials.
2. **Taking a Taxi** — Giving addresses, agreeing the fare before you go.
3. **Asking for Directions** — Right, left, straight; "Is it far?" "How do I get to…?"
4. **Hotels & Check-In** — Booking, room requests, "Breakfast included?"
5. **Transport Words** — Metro, bus, train, car; buying tickets.
6. **Sightseeing Phrases** — "Where is the entrance?", opening hours, photo etiquette.
7. **Dealing with Problems** — Lost, "I need help", finding the police.

## Module 7 — Family & Social Life
Talk about the people who matter, the way Arabs do.

1. **Family Vocabulary** — Mother, father, siblings, children, extended family.
2. **"Do You Have…?"** — Asking about family; the polite way to answer.
3. **Describing People** — Nationalities, occupations, "My brother is a teacher".
4. **Social Invitations** — "Come to my house"; accepting and declining warmly.
5. **Giving Compliments** — Mā shāʾ Allāh and when to say it (important!).
6. **Talking About Yourself** — Age, marital status, where you live, hobbies.

## Module 8 — Work & Business Phrases
Professional Arabic for meetings and emails.

1. **Office Introductions** — Job titles, departments, "I work for…".
2. **Meeting Phrases** — "Let's begin", "I agree", "I have a question".
3. **Scheduling** — Setting appointments; "Are you available on…?"
4. **Phone & Email Basics** — Answering calls, leaving messages, polite openers.
5. **Negotiation Softeners** — In shāʾ Allāh, bismillah — what they really signal in business.
6. **Formal vs Informal Register** — When MSA is expected and when dialect takes over.

## Module 9 — Emergencies & Health
The phrases you hope you'll never need — but must know.

1. **Getting Help** — "Help!", "Call an ambulance!", "Call the police!".
2. **At the Doctor** — "I am sick", "It hurts here", describing symptoms.
3. **At the Pharmacy** — "Do you have…?", dosage words, "I need a prescription".
4. **Lost & Found** — "I lost my passport/wallet", embassy phrases.
5. **Numbers to Remember** — Emergency numbers across major Arab countries.

## Module 10 — Culture, Etiquette & Real Conversations
Speak well *and* act well.

1. **Islamic Greetings** — Ramadan and Eid greetings: Ramaḍān karīm, ʿīd mubārak.
2. **Hospitality Rules** — Accepting tea and food; the right-hand custom.
3. **Dress & Behavior Norms** — Mosque visits, greetings between genders, photography.
4. **Dialect Survival Guide** — Egyptian, Levantine, Gulf: key differences in greetings and daily words.
5. **5 Full Conversations** — Restaurant, taxi, souq, hotel, and small-talk scenarios end-to-end.
6. **Your 60-Second Self-Introduction** — Record yourself; pronunciation checklist.
7. **Next Steps** — How to keep improving: media, apps, and conversation partners.

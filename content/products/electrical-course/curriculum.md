# Home Electrical Basics — Full Curriculum

*4 modules, 18 lessons. Video + text format. Estimated total study time: 8–12 hours.*

---

## Module 1: Electrical Safety Foundations
*The non-negotiable foundation. Complete this module before anything else.*

| # | Lesson | What you'll learn |
|---|---|---|
| 1.1 | How Electricity Works — Voltage, Current & Resistance in Plain Language | The water-pipe analogy; what volts, amps, and ohms actually mean; why current (not voltage) is what hurts you |
| 1.2 | The Golden Safety Rules | Always de-energize • Test before you touch • Lockout/tagout basics for homeowners • The right PPE and why it matters |
| 1.3 | Understanding Your Breaker Panel | What each part does; reading breaker labels; how to safely switch a breaker off and on; what a main breaker is (and why you don't touch it) |
| 1.4 | GFCI & AFCI — What They Are and Why They Matter | How ground-fault and arc-fault protection save lives; where they're required; how to test them monthly |
| 1.5 | Shock & Arc-Flash Awareness + What You Should NEVER DIY | How shocks happen; what arc flash is and why it terrifies professionals; the hard list: service panel mains, aluminum wiring repairs, anything you're unsure of → call a licensed electrician |

**Module 1 outcome:** You can explain how electricity works, shut off power safely, test that it's off, identify your panel's components, and — critically — you know your limits.

## Module 2: Reading Wiring Diagrams & Schematics
*Learn to read the map before you travel the road.*

| # | Lesson | What you'll learn |
|---|---|---|
| 2.1 | How to Read a Residential Wiring Diagram | Diagram conventions; power flow from panel to device; the difference between a wiring diagram and a schematic |
| 2.2 | Common Electrical Symbols | Outlets, switches, lights, panels, and junctions — the symbols you'll see on real drawings |
| 2.3 | Tracing a Circuit from Panel to Outlet | Follow one circuit end-to-end; understand home runs, daisy-chaining, and junction boxes |
| 2.4 | Wire Colors & What They Mean (Canada/CEC) | Black, red, white, green/bare — what each color means in Canadian residential wiring, and why you always verify |
| 2.5 | Switch Loops & 3-Way Switches | Why your hallway light has two switches; how travelers work; reading a 3-way diagram without fear |

**Module 2 outcome:** You can look at a residential wiring diagram, identify every symbol, trace a circuit, and understand what each wire in a box is doing.

## Module 3: Common Home Repairs
*Hands-on skills — every repair starts with Module 1's safety procedure.*

| # | Lesson | What you'll learn |
|---|---|---|
| 3.1 | Replacing a Standard Outlet | Step-by-step receptacle replacement; matching wire to terminal; torque and connection quality — the QA inspector's standard |
| 3.2 | Replacing a Light Switch (Single-Pole & 3-Way) | Safe switch replacement; wiring a 3-way correctly; what to do when the wire colors don't match the textbook |

| # | Lesson | What you'll learn |
|---|---|---|

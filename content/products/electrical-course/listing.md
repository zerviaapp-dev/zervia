## FAQ

**1. Is this safe for beginners?**
The course is *designed* for beginners, and safety is its backbone. Module 1 is entirely about safety foundations — you don't touch a wire until you understand de-energizing, testing, and your limits. That said, electricity is genuinely dangerous, and the course is honest about that: you'll learn exactly which jobs are appropriate for a careful DIYer and which ones require a licensed electrician, no exceptions.

**2. Do I need any experience or background in electrical work?**
No. We start from zero — what voltage, current, and resistance actually mean, in plain language. The only prerequisites are the ability to read instructions carefully and the maturity to follow safety rules every single time.

**3. What tools do I need?**
For the course itself: nothing — you can learn all the theory first. For the hands-on repairs in Module 3, you'll need a basic toolkit: a non-contact voltage tester, a receptacle tester, insulated screwdrivers, wire strippers, and a multimeter. Our included Tools Guide lists exactly what to buy with budget-friendly recommendations — you can get set up for well under $100 CAD.

**4. Does this course certify me as an electrician?**
**No.** This course provides knowledge and skills only — it does not grant any license, certification, or qualification to perform electrical work professionally. Becoming a licensed electrician requires formal apprenticeship and certification through your province's regulatory authority. This course is education for homeowners and DIYers, not a trade credential.
